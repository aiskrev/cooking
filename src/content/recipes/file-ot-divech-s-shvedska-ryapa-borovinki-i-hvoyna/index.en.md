mise:
  - Take the meat out of the refrigerator 40 minutes before cooking.
  - Peel the swede; cut three quarters into pieces for the purée and the rest into 1.5 cm cubes.
  - Have string and a thermometer ready.
technique: Trimming and tying a fillet; searing and butter basting; resting; a swede purée and glazed swede; a wine and stock reduction with blueberries and juniper.
temperature: The fillet is seared over a high heat for 2 minutes on each side and finished by basting or in the oven at 180 °C to 52–54 °C in the centre. It rests for 10 minutes. The purée simmers for 20–25 minutes, the cubes glaze for 10–12 minutes.
balance: The lean meat gets richness from the butter and sauce; the sweetness of the swede and honey is balanced by sour blueberries and juniper, and the spruce tips add a fresh, citrusy resinous note.

steps:
  - title: Lay out the ingredients
    text: Lay out the fillet, swede, blueberries, juniper, spruce tips, butter, wine and stock.
  - title: Prepare the mise en place
    text: Crush the juniper and pepper, chop the shallots and measure out the wine and stock.
  - title: Trim the meat
    text: Remove the silverskin and fat with a thin knife. If serving medallions, trim off the uneven ends.
  - title: Tie
    text: Season the meat with the salt, juniper and pepper and tie it with string every 2–3 cm so it stays round.
  - title: Sear and baste
    text: Sear the fillet in the oil on all sides. Add the butter, thyme and shallots and baste for 4–5 minutes (or finish in the oven). Rest for 10 minutes.
    cue: The thermometer reads 52–54 °C in the centre.
    avoid: Above 58 °C the lean meat dries out.
    time: 10
  - title: Prepare the swede
    text: Simmer the swede pieces in the milk for 20–25 minutes and blend with half the butter. Glaze the cubes in the remaining butter with the honey and a little water for 10–12 minutes until golden.
  - title: Make the sauce
    text: Soften the shallots, add the wine and reduce by two thirds. Add the stock, juniper and half the blueberries and simmer until thick. Strain, add the remaining blueberries and stir in the cold butter.
  - title: Slice and plate
    text: Remove the string and cut the meat into thick medallions. Spoon purée onto the plate, top with the medallions and glazed cubes, pour over the sauce and finish with fresh blueberries and spruce tips.
tips:
  - Spruce tips are picked in May and frozen; out of season use a few rosemary leaves.
  - Swede is also sold as rutabaga; celeriac can replace it.
  - The sauce can be made a day ahead and reheated.
mistakes:
  - Slicing without resting – the juices run out.
  - A purée with too much milk – it spreads across the plate.
  - Too much juniper – the sauce turns bitter.
substitutions:
  - Instead of game – beef fillet.
  - Instead of blueberries – blackberries or redcurrants.

storage: The meat is best at once. The purée and sauce keep in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
