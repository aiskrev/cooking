mise:
  - Take the filo out of the refrigerator 30 minutes ahead.
  - Melt the butter and let it cool slightly.
  - Mix the izvara with the cheese, crumbled finely; grease a tin about 28 cm in diameter.
technique: The rolls are rolled loosely and arranged in a coil. The filling is thick, so the yogurt with the bicarbonate of soda thins it only slightly, and the eggs bind it.
temperature: Oven at 190 °C (170 °C fan), middle shelf, 40–45 minutes. If it browns too quickly, cover with baking paper.
balance: The izvara is sweet and soft, and the cheese provides the salt. Taste the filling before rolling – it should be lightly salted.

steps:
  - title: Make the filling
    text: Whisk the eggs with the yogurt and the bicarbonate of soda, add the izvara and the cheese and stir to a uniform, thick mixture.
    cue: The filling is thick but can be spread with a spoon.
  - title: Roll the rolls
    text: Spread out a filo sheet, brush it with butter and spread a strip of filling along the long side. Roll it loosely and gently gather the roll like an accordion.
    why: Rolling loosely leaves room for the filling to swell and the filo to rise.
    avoid: A tight roll cracks while baking.
  - title: Shape the spiral
    text: Coil the first roll like a snail in the middle of the tin and keep placing the next ones against it. Brush the surface with the remaining butter.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake for 40–45 minutes at 190 °C, until the banitsa has risen and is a dark golden colour.
    cue: The crust is evenly golden and crisp.
    time: 45
  - title: Let it rest
    text: Cover the banitsa with a cloth for 10 minutes, then cut it with a sharp knife.
    why: The filling sets and the pieces come out whole.
    time: 10
tips:
  - If the izvara is very wet, drain it for 30 minutes in muslin – otherwise the filling releases water.
  - Banitsa is best warm, but it is also good cold the next day.
mistakes:
  - Wet izvara – the banitsa turns soft and does not rise.
  - Cutting straight after taking it out – the filling runs out.
substitutions:
  - Ricotta or fresh cheese instead of izvara.
  - A little sugar in the filling for a sweeter version, served with jam.
plating: A piece showing the spiral and the white filling, next to a small bowl of yogurt.
serving: Warm, with ayran, yogurt or a glass of milk.
storage: Up to 2 days in the refrigerator, covered. Reheat in the oven, not in the microwave.
