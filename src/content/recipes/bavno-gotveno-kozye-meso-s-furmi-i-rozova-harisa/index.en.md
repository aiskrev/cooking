  - title: Sear the meat
    text: Sear the shoulder in the oil on all sides until deep golden. Lift it out.
    time: 15
  - title: Braise
    text: Sweat the vegetables and spices, pour in the wine and let it evaporate. Return the meat, add the stock, cover and cook in the oven at 150 °C for 3½–4 hours.
    cue: The meat comes away easily from the bone.
    time: 240
  - title: Make the jus
    text: Strain the liquid, skim off the fat and reduce it to a glossy glaze.
    time: 20
  - title: Glaze the carrots
    text: Cook the carrots in the butter and honey with a little water until tender and glossy. Add the dates for the last minute.
    time: 12
  - title: Plate
    text: Pull the meat into large pieces, coat with the glaze and set beside the carrots and dates. Add dots of harissa mixed with the yoghurt.
