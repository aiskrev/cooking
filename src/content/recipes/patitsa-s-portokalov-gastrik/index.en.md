mise:
  - Take the breasts out of the refrigerator 20 minutes ahead and dry the skin very well.
  - Squeeze the juice of one orange (about 100 ml); peel the other with a knife and cut it into segments without the membranes.
  - Switch the oven on to 180 °C.
technique: The skin is scored and rendered from a cold pan over moderate heat so that the fat melts. The sauce starts with caramelised honey, which is deglazed with vinegar and orange juice, reduced and finished with cold butter.
temperature: "The skin: 8–10 minutes over moderate heat. Oven at 180 °C for 6–8 minutes. For a pink centre, take it out at 54–57 °C; the official recommendation for poultry is 74 °C – at that point the meat is fully cooked and drier."
balance: Duck is fatty and dense; the vinegar and the orange cut through it, and the honey rounds out the acidity. The sauce should be sweet-and-sour, not sweet.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the duck breasts, the oranges, the honey, the vinegar, the butter and the thyme.
    photo: 01-patitsa-stapka-1.webp
  - title: Score the skin
    text: Score the skin in a diamond pattern with a sharp knife, about half a centimetre apart, without cutting into the meat. Season with salt on both sides.
    why: Through the cuts the fat runs out and the skin becomes thin and crisp instead of thick and rubbery.
    photo: 02-patitsa-stapka-2.webp
  - title: Render the skin
    text: Place the breasts skin side down in a cold pan, with no fat, and switch on to moderate heat. Cook for 8–10 minutes, pouring off the rendered fat. Turn them over for 1 minute on the meat side.
    cue: The skin is dark golden, thin and crisp.
    avoid: In a hot pan the skin shrinks and burns before the fat has melted.
    time: 10
    photo: 03-patitsa-stapka-3.webp
  - title: Finish in the oven
    text: Transfer the breasts to a small tray, skin side up, and roast them for 6–8 minutes at 180 °C. Let them rest for 8 minutes on a board.
    cue: The meat springs back under pressure; the thermometer shows 54–57 °C.
    time: 8
    photo: 04-patitsa-stapka-4.webp
  - title: Make the gastrique
    text: Pour the fat out of the pan. Add the honey and let it boil for 1–2 minutes until it darkens. Pour in the vinegar and then the orange juice, add the thyme and cook until the sauce becomes a syrup. Take it off the heat and whisk in the cold butter in pieces.
    cue: The sauce coats the back of a spoon and is glossy.
    avoid: Vinegar in hot honey gives off a sharp vapour – keep your face back.
    photo: 05-patitsa-stapka-5.webp
  - title: Slice and serve
    text: Slice the breasts on the diagonal, fan them out on a plate, spoon over the sauce and add the orange segments.
    photo: 06-patitsa-stapka-6.webp
tips:
  - The rendered duck fat is excellent for roast potatoes – do not throw it away.
  - "The rest is essential: without it the meat loses its juice when cut."
mistakes:
  - A hot pan from the start – the skin burns, and a layer of fat is left under it.
  - Boiling the sauce after the butter has gone in – it separates.
substitutions:
  - Wine vinegar instead of cider vinegar; sugar instead of honey.
  - A spoonful of orange liqueur in the sauce.
plating: The slices fanned out, skin side up, the sauce beside the meat, not over the skin; a few orange segments.
serving: With celeriac purée or potatoes and a glass of red wine.
storage: Best straight away. The sauce can be prepared in advance and warmed without boiling.
