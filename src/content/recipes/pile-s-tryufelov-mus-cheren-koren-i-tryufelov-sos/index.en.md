mise:
  - Chill the food-processor bowl and blade in the refrigerator – the mousse must stay cold.
  - Prepare a bowl of water and lemon juice for the peeled salsify, which darkens quickly.
  - Carefully loosen the skin on the breasts with your fingers without tearing it from the edges.
technique: A cold chicken mousse (farce), piping under the skin, a short chill, searing skin-side down and butter-basting, caramelising salsify, a reduced sauce with cream and truffle.
temperature: The mousse is made below 8 °C so it does not split. The breasts are seared skin-side down over medium-high heat for 6–8 minutes and finished in the oven at 180 °C to 65 °C at the core. The salsify is sautéed over medium heat for 10–12 minutes.
balance: Truffle and cream are rich and earthy, so the wine, the lemon in the salsify and the tarragon bring freshness. Salt moderately – truffle loses its aroma when over-salted.

steps:
  - title: Lay out the ingredients
    text: Arrange the breasts, the chicken for the mousse, the truffle, salsify, butter, cream, stock and herbs.
    photo: 01-pile-stapka-1.webp
  - title: Make the mousse
    text: Blend the cold chicken with the salt to a paste, add the egg white, then the cream in a thin stream. Pass through a sieve and add the finely chopped truffle. Fill a piping bag.
    cue: The mousse is smooth, glossy and holds its shape.
    avoid: Do not blend for long – the mousse warms up and splits.
    time: 10
    photo: 02-pile-stapka-2.webp
  - title: Fill the breasts
    text: Slide the truffle slices under the skin, then pipe a thin, even layer of mousse. Smooth it with your fingers over the skin. Chill for 30 minutes.
    why: Chilling sets the mousse so it does not leak during searing.
    time: 30
    photo: 03-pile-stapka-3.webp
  - title: Pipe evenly
    text: Check that the mousse reaches the edges evenly – thicker spots cook more slowly.
    photo: 04-pile-stapka-4.webp
  - title: Make the sauce
    text: Sweat the shallot in a little butter, pour in the wine and reduce almost completely. Add the stock and reduce by half, then the cream and tarragon. Simmer to a creamy consistency, strain and add the truffle.
    cue: The sauce coats the spoon.
    time: 20
    photo: 05-pile-stapka-5.webp
  - title: Sauté the salsify
    text: Peel the salsify, cut into 8 cm batons and drop them into the lemon water. Pat dry and sauté in the butter with the thyme over medium heat for 10–12 minutes, turning.
    cue: The batons are golden and tender but keep their shape.
    time: 12
    photo: 06-pile-stapka-6.webp
  - title: Caramelise the salsify
    text: For the last few minutes raise the heat until the butter turns nutty and season with salt.
    photo: 07-pile-stapka-7.webp
  - title: Sear the breasts
    text: Place the breasts skin-side down in a pan with the oil and sear for 6–8 minutes. Turn, add the butter and thyme and baste with the foam. Finish in the oven at 180 °C to 65 °C at the core and rest for 5 minutes.
    cue: The skin is crisp and golden and the core reads 65 °C.
    time: 18
    photo: 08-pile-stapka-8.webp
  - title: Finish the sauce and serve
    text: Warm the sauce and whisk in the cold butter off the heat. Cut each breast in half to show the layer of mousse. Arrange with the salsify and pour over the sauce.
    photo: 09-pile-stapka-9.webp
tips:
  - Check the seasoning of the mousse by poaching a teaspoon in hot water.
  - Peel salsify wearing gloves – its sap is sticky and stains.
  - Keep truffle trimmings for the sauce – the aroma is strongest when not heated for long.
mistakes:
  - Warm mousse – it splits and leaks during cooking.
  - Skin torn when loosening it – the mousse runs out.
  - Overcooked chicken – the mousse protects it, but above 70 °C the breasts dry out.
substitutions:
  - Instead of salsify – parsnip or white asparagus.
  - Instead of fresh truffle – truffle paste in the mousse and the sauce.
plating: "A large white plate: a chicken breast cut in two to show the layer, a bundle of salsify beside it and sauce around the meat."
serving: With a white Burgundy (Chardonnay) or champagne.
storage: Best eaten straight away; the breasts keep for 1 day in the refrigerator and are reheated gently in the oven.
