mise:
  - Peel the potatoes and cut them into cubes about 1 cm on a side; keep them in water so that they do not darken.
  - Finely chop the onion and the carrot and grate or chop the tomatoes.
  - Switch the oven on to 190 °C and grease a baking tin of about 25 × 35 cm with oil.
technique: The mince is fried until its water has evaporated and it begins to brown – that is where the deep flavour comes from. The moussaka is baked first without the topping so that the potatoes soften, and only then is it covered.
temperature: "Oven at 190 °C (170 °C fan): 40 minutes under foil, then another 15–20 minutes with the topping, without foil. The mince must be completely cooked – no pink areas."
balance: The potatoes bring sweetness, the mince richness, and the paprika and the savory the aroma. The yogurt in the topping and on the side of the plate lightens the fat.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the potatoes, the minced meat, the onion, the carrot, the tomatoes, the eggs, the yogurt and the spices.
  - title: Cut the vegetables
    text: Cut the potatoes into cubes of 1 cm and the onion and the carrot finely. Drain the potatoes and pat them dry.
    why: Cubes of the same size soften at the same time; with different sizes some of them stay hard.
  - title: Fry the mince
    text: Heat the oil in a wide frying pan over high heat. Add the mince and fry it for 8–10 minutes, breaking up the lumps with a wooden spoon.
    why: As long as there is water in the pan, the mince boils. The flavour appears when the water has evaporated and the meat begins to brown.
    cue: The mince is in crumbs, with no pink colour, and what sizzles in the pan is fat, not water.
    time: 10
  - title: Add the vegetables and the spices
    text: Lower the heat to medium, add the onion and the carrot and cook for 5 minutes. Add the tomatoes, the salt, the black pepper and the savory and leave for another 5 minutes. Take the pan off the heat and only then stir in the paprika.
    avoid: Paprika burns in seconds in hot fat and turns bitter – that is why it goes in at the end, off the heat.
    time: 10
  - title: Layer and bake
    text: Spread half of the potatoes in the tin, salt them lightly, cover with the mince and finish with the remaining potatoes. Pour in the hot water, cover with foil and bake for 40 minutes.
    cue: A knife goes into the potatoes without resistance, and the liquid is almost absorbed.
    time: 40
  - title: Whisk the topping
    text: About ten minutes before the end, beat the eggs with the yogurt, the flour, the melted butter and a pinch of salt to a smooth mixture without lumps.
    why: The flour stabilises the topping – without it, it curdles and releases water.
  - title: Pour over the topping
    text: Take the tin out, remove the foil and pour the topping evenly over the potatoes. Shake the tin gently to distribute it.
  - title: Finish baking
    text: Return the moussaka to the oven for another 15–20 minutes, without foil, until the topping sets and forms a golden crust.
    cue: The topping is set, slightly risen and golden in patches; when shaken, the middle does not wobble.
    time: 20
  - title: Let it rest
    text: Leave the moussaka for 15 minutes in the tin before cutting it.
    why: The layers firm up and the pieces come out whole, with a clean cut.
    time: 15
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve a warm piece with a spoonful of yogurt and finely chopped parsley.
tips:
  - Choose potatoes for boiling and baking that keep their shape; very floury ones fall apart into mush.
  - If the mince is lean, add another spoonful of oil – dry mince makes the moussaka crumbly.
  - The topping is whisked at the last moment; left to stand, it separates.
mistakes:
  - Pouring the topping over raw potatoes – the topping bakes long before the potatoes soften.
  - Too much water – the moussaka turns watery and the layers fall apart.
  - Cutting straight after taking it out – the piece does not hold its shape.
substitutions:
  - Beef mince only gives a lighter flavour; add a spoonful of oil, since it is drier.
  - Instead of savory – dried spearmint or thyme.
  - For a thicker crust, sprinkle the topping with 50 g of grated kashkaval before the final baking.
plating: A whole piece with the layers visible, a spoonful of yogurt on the side and a sprig of parsley.
serving: Warm, with yogurt and shopska salad.
storage: Up to 3 days in the refrigerator, covered. Reheat in an oven at 170 °C to restore the crust.
