mise:
  - Soak the almonds for the cream in warm water for 1 hour.
  - Remove the outer leaves of the cauliflower but keep the small inner ones – they roast crisp.
  - Toast the cumin and coriander in a dry pan and crush them.
  - Halve the grapes.
technique: Blanching and roasting a whole cauliflower with repeated basting in spiced butter; an almond cream; a sauce of brown butter and stock; fried curry leaves.
temperature: The cauliflower is blanched for 6–8 minutes in salted water and roasted at 200 °C for about 50–60 minutes, basted every 10 minutes. It is done when a knife goes into the core without resistance.
balance: The spiced butter brings warmth and bitterness, the almond cream softness, the grapes acidity and sweetness. Lemon in the sauce keeps it from feeling heavy.

steps:
  - title: Lay out the ingredients
    text: Lay out the cauliflower, almonds, grapes, spices, curry leaves, shallots, garlic and butter.
  - title: Prepare the mise en place
    text: Mix the crushed spices with the turmeric and paprika. Peel the shallots and garlic, and set out the almonds and grapes in small bowls.
  - title: Prepare the cauliflower
    text: Trim the stalk so the cauliflower stands steady. Blanch it whole in salted water for 6–8 minutes and dry it well.
    why: Blanching cooks the core, while the oven takes care of the crust.
    time: 8
  - title: Roast with spiced butter
    text: Melt the butter with the olive oil and spices. Brush the cauliflower generously, put it in a roasting pan with the shallots and garlic, and roast at 200 °C for 50–60 minutes, basting with the butter every 10 minutes.
    cue: The surface is deep golden, charred in places, and a knife slides in freely.
    time: 55
  - title: Make the almond cream
    text: Drain the almonds and blend them with the warm stock, lemon juice and salt on high speed for 3–4 minutes until smooth.
  - title: Make the sauce and curry leaves
    text: Brown the butter, add the stock and the fat from the pan, simmer for 2 minutes and strain; season with lemon. Fry the curry leaves in hot oil for 10 seconds and drain.
  - title: Plate
    text: Spread almond cream on the plate, set the cauliflower on top and spoon over the sauce. Finish with the grapes, toasted flaked almonds, curry leaves and drops of herb oil.
tips:
  - Smaller cauliflowers (400–500 g each) roast faster and make a single portion.
  - Curry leaves are sold in Asian grocers; dried ones lack the aroma.
  - Leftover spiced butter is excellent with rice or lentils.
mistakes:
  - Skipping the blanching – the crust burns before the core softens.
  - Basting too rarely – the surface dries out instead of caramelising.
  - Under-blended almond cream – it stays grainy.
substitutions:
  - Instead of green grapes – pomegranate seeds.
  - Instead of almonds – cashews.
  - "Vegan version: olive oil instead of butter."
plating: "A wide plate: almond cream, the cauliflower in the centre, sauce around it, grapes, almonds and curry leaves on top."
serving: "Warm, as a vegetarian main course for two or a sharing plate for four. Suitable non-alcoholic pairing: lassi, or mint and lime with sparkling water."
storage: The roasted cauliflower keeps in the refrigerator for up to 2 days and is reheated in the oven. The cream keeps for up to 3 days.
