mise:
  - Take the chicken out 20 minutes ahead and dry the skin very well.
  - Slice the mushrooms and chop the shallot and the garlic finely.
  - Switch the oven on to 180 °C.
technique: "The skin is seared over moderate heat until the fat under it melts; the meat is finished in the oven. The sauce is made in a separate pan: the mushrooms are browned, the wine is reduced and the cream binds it."
temperature: Oven at 180 °C, 10–12 minutes. The chicken is done at 74 °C in the thickest part.
balance: The mushrooms and the cream are soft and rich; the wine gives acidity and the thyme aroma. The sauce should not be heavy – it should flow.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the chicken breasts, the mushrooms, the shallot, the cream, the wine, the butter and the thyme.
  - title: Season
    text: Season the breasts with salt on both sides and sprinkle with black pepper.
  - title: Sear the skin
    text: Heat the oil in an ovenproof frying pan. Put the breasts in skin side down and cook for 6–7 minutes over moderate heat, without moving them. Turn them over and add half of the butter and the thyme.
    cue: The skin is evenly golden and comes away from the pan by itself.
    avoid: Over high heat the skin burns before the fat under it has melted.
    time: 7
  - title: Finish in the oven
    text: Put the pan in the oven for 10–12 minutes. Take the breasts out onto a board and let them rest for 5 minutes.
    cue: The thermometer shows 74 °C, and the juice is clear.
    time: 12
  - title: Make the sauce
    text: While the chicken cooks, brown the mushrooms in the remaining butter in another pan over high heat until their water has evaporated and they turn brown. Add the shallot and the garlic for 1 minute, pour in the wine and let it reduce almost completely. Pour in the cream, let it simmer for 2–3 minutes and season with salt.
    cue: The sauce clings to the mushrooms and coats the back of a spoon.
    time: 10
  - title: Slice and serve
    text: Slice the breasts on the diagonal into thick slices, arrange them on the sauce and spoon over the juice from the board.
tips:
  - Mushrooms are salted at the end – salted early, they release water and boil.
  - The handle of the pan stays hot for a long time after it comes out – wrap it in a cloth.
mistakes:
  - Leaving it in the oven "to be safe" – the breasts dry out within minutes.
  - Cream added to wine that has not reduced enough – the sauce stays sour and thin.
substitutions:
  - Boneless chicken thighs – they finish in 15–18 minutes.
  - Wild mushrooms in season; a spoonful of mustard in the sauce.
plating: The sauce at the base of the plate, the slices on it skin side up, a few thyme leaves.
serving: With mashed potatoes, rice or fresh pasta.
storage: Up to 2 days in the refrigerator. Reheat the meat in the sauce over low heat.
