mise:
  - Set aside 12 small florets for roasting; finely chop the rest of the cauliflower for the purée.
  - Dry the fish well and leave it uncovered in the refrigerator for 30 minutes – the skin crisps better.
  - Roughly chop the hazelnuts.
technique: A purée of cauliflower cooked in milk; roasted cauliflower florets; fish seared skin side down and basted with butter.
temperature: The fish cooks for 4–5 minutes skin side down over a medium-high heat and 30–60 seconds on the other side – to 50 °C in the centre. The florets roast at 210 °C for 15–18 minutes.
balance: The sweet purée and delicate fish get salt from the roe, bitterness from the roasted cauliflower, and richness and aroma from the hazelnuts.

steps:
  - title: Cook the purée
    text: Simmer the finely chopped cauliflower in the milk over a low heat for 15–18 minutes. Blend with the butter and some of the milk to a silky purée and season.
    cue: The purée is smooth and holds its shape on the spoon.
    time: 18
  - title: Roast the florets
    text: Toss the florets with oil and salt and roast at 210 °C for 15–18 minutes until golden.
    time: 18
  - title: Sear the fish
    text: Salt the skin and lay it in a hot pan with the oil. Press gently with a spatula for the first 20 seconds and cook for 4–5 minutes without moving it.
    avoid: Do not turn the fish too early – the skin will stay in the pan.
    time: 5
  - title: Baste with butter
    text: Turn the fish, add the butter and thyme and baste for 30–60 seconds. Drain on paper.
  - title: Plate
    text: Spoon purée into the centre of the plate, set the fish on top skin side up and the florets around it. Finish with roe, hazelnuts, drops of herb oil and herbs.
tips:
  - If the skin curls, make 2–3 shallow cuts.
  - Add the roe at the last moment – heat makes it cloudy.
  - The purée can be made ahead and reheated.
mistakes:
  - Wet skin – the fish steams instead of searing.
  - A purée with too much milk – it runs across the plate.
  - Overcooking – the cod falls apart into flakes.
substitutions:
  - Instead of cod – hake, sea bass or haddock.
  - Instead of salmon roe – trout roe.

serving: "At once. Suitable non-alcoholic pairings: cold elderflower tea, or sparkling water with lemon and mint."
storage: Cook the fish just before serving. The purée keeps in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.
