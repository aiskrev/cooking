mise:
  - Pat the pigeons dry and remove any feather stubs. Tie the legs.
  - Roast the beetroot in foil at 190 °C for 45 minutes, peel and cut.
  - Chop the shallots and crush the juniper.
technique: Searing a whole bird with butter, finishing the breasts, a red wine sauce with juniper and blackberries, beetroot roasted in foil.
temperature: Sear for 2–3 minutes on each side, oven at 200 °C for 6–8 minutes; 54–56 °C in the breasts. Rest for 8 minutes (up to 58 °C).
balance: Dark poultry meat wants sweetness and acidity – vinegar, blackberries, honey.
