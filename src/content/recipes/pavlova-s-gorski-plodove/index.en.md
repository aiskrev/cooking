mise:
  - The egg whites must be at room temperature and the bowl grease-free.
  - Draw a circle 20 cm in diameter on baking paper.
  - Switch the oven on to 120 °C (100 °C fan).
technique: The sugar is added gradually to the whites and whisked until completely dissolved. The starch and the acid stabilise the meringue, and slow baking and cooling in the oven protect it from cracks.
temperature: Oven at 120 °C (100 °C fan), 80–90 minutes, then switched off and left with the door slightly ajar for at least an hour.
balance: The meringue is very sweet, so the fruit must be sharp – raspberries, passion fruit and kiwi.

steps:
  - title: Whisk the whites
    text: Whisk the whites with the salt to soft peaks. Add the sugar a spoonful at a time, whisking on high speed for 8–10 minutes.
    cue: The meringue is thick and glossy, and no sugar can be felt between the fingers.
    time: 10
  - title: Add the starch
    text: Add the cornflour, the lemon juice and the vanilla and fold them in gently.
  - title: Shape the nest
    text: Pour the meringue onto the marked circle. Smooth the edges and make a hollow in the middle with a spoon.
    why: The hollow holds the cream and the fruit.
  - title: Bake slowly
    text: Bake for 80–90 minutes at 120 °C. Switch off the oven and leave the pavlova inside for at least an hour.
    avoid: Do not open the oven – a sudden change in temperature produces cracks.
    time: 90
  - title: Decorate
    text: Whip the cream with the icing sugar. Just before serving, put the pavlova on a plate, spread it with the cream and arrange the fruit and the passion fruit on top.
    why: If the cream goes on in advance, the meringue softens.
tips:
  - The baked meringue keeps in an airtight container for up to two days.
  - The sugar is added a spoonful at a time – this way it dissolves completely and the meringue does not weep.
mistakes:
  - Grease in the bowl – the whites will not whip.
  - Adding the sugar all at once – the meringue stays liquid.
substitutions:
  - Coconut milk instead of cream.
  - Mango or peaches when they are in season.
plating: The pavlova on a light-coloured plate, the fruit on top, passion fruit by the spoonful and drops of red syrup.
serving: Chilled, immediately after decorating.
storage: The meringue – up to 2 days in an airtight container; once decorated, it does not keep.
