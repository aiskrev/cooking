mise:
  - Peel the vegetables and cut them into large pieces.
  - Grate the tomatoes or chop them finely.
technique: The meat is fried to colour, the vegetables are sweated, and everything is brought to maturity covered over low heat.
temperature: After the initial boil the heat is low – the sauce barely bubbles. Chicken is safe at 74 °C at least, but the drumsticks turn tender after a longer braise.
balance: The sweetness of the carrots and the peppers, the acidity of the tomatoes and the soft, juicy meat.

steps:
  - title: Brown the drumsticks
    text: Season the drumsticks with salt and brown them in the hot oil for 4–5 minutes on each side, to a golden colour. Take them out.
    why: Browned meat gives a richer flavour to the sauce.
    time: 10
  - title: Sweat the vegetables
    text: In the same pot sweat the onion and the carrots for 5 minutes. Add the peppers and fry them for another 2 minutes.
    time: 7
  - title: Add the paprika and the tomatoes
    text: Take the pot off the heat, add the paprika and the tomato purée, stir and add the grated tomatoes.
    avoid: The paprika must not burn – it turns bitter.
  - title: Braise
    text: Return the drumsticks, add the potatoes and the water. Bring to the boil, cover and leave on low heat for 35–40 minutes.
    cue: The meat comes away easily from the bone and the potatoes are soft.
    time: 40
  - title: Rest and serve
    text: Take it off the heat and leave it for 10 minutes. Sprinkle with parsley and serve in deep plates.
    time: 10
tips:
  - If the sauce is thin, leave the last 10 minutes without a lid.
  - Taste it for salt before serving.
mistakes:
  - Too high a heat – the meat tightens up and the liquid boils away.
  - Vegetables cut too small – they fall apart.
substitutions:
  - Chicken thighs instead of drumsticks.
  - Courgette or aubergine instead of part of the potatoes.
plating: A drumstick with vegetables and sauce in a deep bowl, sprinkled with fresh parsley.
serving: With sliced fresh bread for dipping in the sauce.
storage: Up to 3 days in the refrigerator; the next day it is even tastier.
