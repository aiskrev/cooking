mise:
  - Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  - Cut the pumpkin into thick wedges and remove the seeds.
  - Lay out a sheet of baking paper for the praline.
technique: Roasting and caramelising, a dark vegetable jus from roasted vegetables, a caramel praline and frying leaves.
temperature: The pumpkin is roasted at 200 °C for 35–45 minutes. The vegetables for the jus are roasted at 220 °C for 30 minutes. The caramel for the praline is taken to 170 °C (light amber).
balance: The sweetness of the pumpkin is balanced by the salty Comté and the bitter note of the dark jus.

steps:
  - title: Lay out the ingredients
    text: Lay out the pumpkin, Comté, sage, pumpkin seeds and vegetables.
    photo: 01-pechena-stapka-1.webp
  - title: Make the vegetable jus
    text: Roast the chopped vegetables with the tomato purée at 220 °C for 30 minutes. Deglaze with the wine, add the water and simmer for 40 minutes. Strain and reduce to 150 ml. Whisk in the cold butter.
    cue: The jus is dark and glossy.
    time: 70
    photo: 02-pechena-stapka-2.webp
  - title: Make the praline
    text: Toast the pumpkin seeds. Caramelise the sugar to light amber, add the seeds and salt, pour onto the paper and leave to cool. Break into pieces.
    time: 15
    photo: 03-pechena-stapka-3.webp
  - title: Roast the pumpkin
    text: Toss the pumpkin with the olive oil, season and roast for 35–45 minutes at 200 °C. At the end, add the butter and sage and baste.
    cue: The pumpkin is soft and the edges caramelised.
    time: 45
  - title: Fry the sage
    text: Fry the leaves for 10 seconds in oil at 170 °C and drain.
    time: 1
  - title: Plate
    text: Place the pumpkin on the plate, pour the jus around it, grate Comté over the top and add praline and fried sage.
tips:
  - Hokkaido does not need peeling – the skin roasts and softens.
  - The praline keeps in an airtight jar for up to a week.
mistakes:
  - Pumpkin cut too thin – it dries out before it caramelises.
  - Dark caramel – the praline turns bitter.
substitutions:
  - Instead of Comté – aged Gruyère.
  - Instead of pumpkin seeds – hazelnuts.
