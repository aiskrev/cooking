steps:
  - title: Cut and season
    text: Cut the meat into strips or bites, season with salt and sprinkle with black pepper. Leave for 15 minutes.
    time: 15
  - title: Make the batter
    text: Whisk the egg with the flour, the paprika, the turmeric and the water to a thick, smooth batter.
    cue: The batter coats the meat in a thin even layer and does not run off.
  - title: Coat
    text: Dip the bites in the batter, letting the excess drain off.
  - title: Fry
    text: Heat the oil over medium heat and fry the bites in batches, 4–5 minutes on each side, to a golden colour. Drain on kitchen paper.
    cue: Inside, the meat is white, with no pink, and the juice is clear.
    avoid: Do not fry too many pieces at once.
    time: 10
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve immediately, with a slice of lemon and a salad.
tips:
  - Cut into pieces of equal size, they cook evenly.
  - A yogurt and garlic sauce goes with them perfectly.
mistakes:
  - Wet meat – the batter slides off.
  - High heat – the coating darkens and the inside is raw.
substitutions:
  - Boneless chicken thighs.
  - Baking in the oven at 200 °C for about 20 minutes.
plating: The bites in a small pile on a warm plate, with a slice of lemon.
