mise:
  - Soak the beans overnight in plenty of very cold water – at least 8 hours.
  - Cut the onion, the carrot and the pepper into small dice; grate the tomatoes.
technique: The first water is discarded, the beans are simmered slowly with the vegetables, and the zaprazhka of oil and paprika is added right at the end.
temperature: Quiet simmering with the lid ajar, 75–90 minutes depending on the beans. The beans must be completely soft – undercooked beans are heavy on the stomach.
balance: The beans are sweetish and dense; the tomatoes freshen them, the spearmint gives the characteristic aroma, and the hot pepper is for whoever wants it.

steps:
  - title: Soak the beans
    text: Cover the beans with plenty of cold water and leave them to soak overnight.
    why: Soaked beans cook faster and more evenly.
  - title: Discard the first water
    text: Drain the beans and rinse them. Cover them with fresh water, bring to the boil, boil for 5 minutes and discard this water too.
    why: The first water carries away the substances that make beans heavy.
  - title: Cut the vegetables
    text: Cut the onion, the carrot and the pepper into small dice.
  - title: Sweat the vegetables
    text: Heat three spoonfuls of the oil in a pot and sweat the vegetables for 5–6 minutes over medium heat.
    time: 6
  - title: Add the beans and the water
    text: Put the beans in with the vegetables and pour in the water – it should cover them by about three fingers.
  - title: Simmer
    text: Let it simmer with the lid ajar for 75–90 minutes. Top up with hot water if the level drops.
    cue: The beans squash easily between the fingers but are still whole.
    avoid: Do not salt yet – in salted water the beans soften more slowly.
    time: 85
  - title: Add the tomatoes and the spices
    text: When the beans are soft, add the tomatoes, the savory, the spearmint and the salt. Simmer for another 10 minutes.
    why: The acidity of the tomatoes also stops the softening, so they go in at the end.
    time: 10
  - title: Make the zaprazhka
    text: Heat the remaining oil in a small frying pan, take it off the heat and stir the paprika into it for a few seconds.
    avoid: Paprika burns instantly – the pan must be off the heat.
  - title: Pour in the zaprazhka
    text: Pour the zaprazhka into the soup, stir and let it boil for 2–3 minutes. Taste for salt.
    time: 3
  - title: Serve
    text: Leave the soup for 10 minutes under the lid and serve with a hot pepper and bread.
tips:
  - For a thicker soup, mash a ladleful of beans with a fork and return it to the pot.
  - Old beans take much longer to cook – buy from the new harvest.
mistakes:
  - Salting early or adding tomatoes early – the beans stay hard.
  - A burnt zaprazhka – the soup turns bitter.
substitutions:
  - Fresh spearmint instead of dried – a handful, added in the last minute.
  - For a non-lenten version, cook a piece of smoked ribs with the beans.
plating: In a clay bowl, with a pinch of spearmint and a hot pepper on the side.
serving: With fresh bread, a hot pepper and pickles.
storage: Up to 3 days in the refrigerator. When reheated the soup thickens – thin it with a little water.
