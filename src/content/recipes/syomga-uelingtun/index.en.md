mise:
  - Blanch the spinach, cool it and squeeze it out completely, then chop it.
  - Prepare two thin crêpes (with flour, egg and milk).
  - Pat the salmon dry, salt it and sprinkle it with pepper.
technique: Dry salmon is wrapped in spinach and cheese and rolled in a crêpe, which absorbs the moisture and keeps the pastry crisp. The roll is chilled before the pastry.
temperature: Oven at 200 °C (180 °C fan), 25 minutes. For juicy salmon take it out at 52–54 °C; the official recommendation for fish is 63 °C – at that point it is drier and fully cooked.
balance: The oily salmon, the lemon zest and the sharp butter sauce are in perfect balance. The spinach adds greenness and freshness.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the spinach layer
    text: Mix the squeezed spinach with the cream cheese, the lemon zest, salt and pepper to a smooth paste.
    cue: The paste is thick and releases no liquid.
  - title: Wrap the salmon
    text: Lay the crêpes out on cling film, spread them with the spinach paste, put the salmon on and wrap tightly. Chill for 30 minutes.
    time: 30
  - title: Wrap in pastry
    text: Roll out the pastry, wrap the chilled roll in it and seal the edges. Brush with the yolk and score a pattern.
    avoid: Do not pierce the pastry – juice leaks out through the holes.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake for 25 minutes at 200 °C, until the pastry is dark golden.
    cue: The thermometer shows 52–54 °C for pink, juicy salmon.
    time: 25
  - title: Make the sauce
    text: Sweat the finely chopped shallot, pour in the wine and reduce it by half. Add the cream and cook for 3 minutes. Off the heat, whisk in the cold butter and the lemon juice.
    time: 8
  - title: Rest and slice
    text: Leave the roll for 10 minutes, slice it with a serrated knife and serve with the sauce and green beans.
    time: 10
tips:
  - Squeeze the spinach out completely – a wet layer is the main cause of soggy pastry.
  - The roll can be prepared up to the pastry stage the day before.
mistakes:
  - Warm salmon in the pastry – the pastry melts.
  - Overbaking – the salmon turns dry.
substitutions:
  - Trout instead of salmon.
  - If you have no crêpes, sprinkle the spinach paste with a thin layer of breadcrumbs – they absorb the moisture.
plating: Two thick pieces showing the layers, on the butter sauce, with green beans and mash beside them.
serving: With mashed potatoes and white wine.
storage: Best fresh; the leftovers – up to 1 day in the refrigerator.
