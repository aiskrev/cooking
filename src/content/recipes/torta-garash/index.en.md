mise:
  - Draw four circles 20 cm in diameter on baking paper.
  - The egg whites must be at room temperature and the bowl dry and grease-free.
  - Chop the chocolate finely.
technique: The walnuts are folded into a stable meringue with a spatula, with movements from the bottom upwards, so that the air does not collapse. The cream is a ganache – chocolate melted in hot cream – which is chilled and whipped.
temperature: "Layers: 170 °C (150 °C fan), 12–15 minutes. The glaze is poured at about 32–35 °C – lukewarm against the lip, but still liquid."
balance: The cake is sweet and dense, so the chocolate must be dark. The pinch of salt in the layers brings out the walnuts.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the walnuts, the chocolate, the cream, the butter, the sugar and the eggs. Separate the whites.
    photo: 01-torta-stapka-1.webp
  - title: Grind the walnuts
    text: Grind the walnuts finely together with the flour, in short pulses.
    why: The flour absorbs the oil of the walnuts and they do not turn into a paste.
    avoid: Do not grind for long without a pause – the walnuts release oil.
    photo: 02-torta-stapka-2.webp
  - title: Whisk the meringue and add the walnuts
    text: Whisk the whites with the salt to soft peaks, add the sugar in three additions and whisk to a thick gloss. Add the walnuts in two additions, folding carefully with a spatula from the bottom upwards.
    cue: The meringue stands in stiff peaks, and the finished mixture is thick and glossy.
    avoid: Vigorous stirring drives out the air and the layers come out flat and hard.
    photo: 03-torta-stapka-3.webp
  - title: Shape the layers
    text: Spread the mixture over the marked circles and smooth it with a spatula into an even layer about half a centimetre thick.
    photo: 04-torta-stapka-4.webp
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake the layers for 12–15 minutes at 170 °C, two at a time. Let them cool completely on the paper and only then peel it off.
    cue: The layers are light golden and dry to the touch.
    time: 15
    photo: 05-torta-stapka-5.webp
  - title: Prepare the cream
    text: Heat the cream for the cream layer to the boil, pour it over the chocolate and after a minute stir until smooth. Chill in the refrigerator for about an hour and whip with a mixer for 1–2 minutes.
    cue: The cream has turned paler and holds its shape on the spoon.
    avoid: An overwhipped ganache curdles and turns grainy – stop as soon as it thickens.
    photo: 06-torta-stapka-6.webp
  - title: Assemble
    text: Put the first layer on a cardboard base or a plate, spread it with a quarter of the cream and repeat with the rest. Spread the last quarter thinly on top and on the sides. Chill the cake for 1 hour.
    time: 60
    photo: 07-torta-stapka-7.webp
  - title: Glaze
    text: Heat the cream for the glaze, melt the chocolate and the butter in it and let it cool to lukewarm. Pour the glaze into the middle of the cake and let it run down the sides by itself.
    why: On a cold cake the glaze sets quickly and stays smooth.
    avoid: Do not smooth it with a knife more than once or twice – marks are left.
    photo: 08-torta-stapka-8.webp
  - title: Chill and slice
    text: Decorate with walnuts and leave the cake in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours, ideally overnight. Cut with a knife dipped in hot water and dried.
    photo: 09-torta-stapka-9.webp
tips:
  - If the layers stick to the paper, dampen its back lightly and wait a minute.
  - The leftover yolks are useful for crème caramel, for kozunak or for homemade noodles.
mistakes:
  - Assembling with warm layers – the cream melts and runs out.
  - Overheated chocolate – the glaze loses its gloss.
substitutions:
  - Hazelnuts instead of walnuts – the flavour is milder.
  - A spoonful of rum or cognac in the cream.
plating: A narrow piece on a white plate, with half a walnut on top.
serving: Lightly chilled, with unsweetened coffee.
storage: Up to 4 days in the refrigerator, covered. Take it out 20 minutes before serving.
allergens:
