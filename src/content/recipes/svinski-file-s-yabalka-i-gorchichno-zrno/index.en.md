mise:
  - Take the meat out 30 minutes ahead, pat dry and salt.
  - Dice the apple and the cucumber into 5 mm cubes and season them with the vinegar and honey.
  - Cut the celeriac into cubes and prepare the shallot.
technique: Searing marbled pork, roasting to pink, puréeing celeriac, an acidic garnish of apple and cucumber, a jus-based sauce.
temperature: Sear for 2–3 minutes on each side over high heat, then oven at 180 °C for 8–12 minutes to 62–63 °C at the core. Rest for 8 minutes.
balance: The fat of the pork needs the acidity of the apple and vinegar and the sharpness of the mustard.

tips:
  - Pork does not need to be well done at 63 °C – let the rest do the work.
  - The garnish is better at room temperature.
mistakes:
  - Overcooking the meat – it becomes dry.
  - Too much sweet garnish.
substitutions:
  - Instead of pork – veal rump.
  - Instead of celeriac – parsnip purée.
plating: Purée as a stripe, meat on top, green cubes and mustard seeds around, the sauce along the rim.
serving: With a glass of white wine (Riesling).
storage: Up to 2 days in the refrigerator, without the garnish of fresh vegetables.
