mise:
  - Cut the meat into 3 cm pieces and pat it dry.
  - Dice the aubergine, salt it for 20 minutes, then rinse and pat dry.
  - If the beans come from a jar, rinse them.
technique: The meat is seared and then layered with the raw vegetables and the cooked beans. The tomato sauce and the vegetables' own juice are the only liquid.
temperature: The clay pot goes into a cold oven. Switch it on to 180 °C (160 °C fan) and bake for about 2 hours from switching on, under a lid, then 10 minutes without it.
balance: The olives are salty, the tomatoes sharp, and the potatoes and the beans absorb the sauce. Salt moderately and taste at the end.

tips:
  - If you use dried beans, soak them overnight and cook them beforehand – in the acidic tomato sauce they do not soften.
  - A new clay pot is soaked in water before its first baking.
mistakes:
  - Raw beans straight into the guvech – they stay hard.
  - Too much water – the vegetables boil and the sauce does not thicken.
substitutions:
  - Beef or lamb instead of pork – with 20–30 minutes longer baking.
  - "Lenten version: without meat, with double the beans."
plating: Straight in the clay pot, in the middle of the table.
serving: With fresh bread, shopska salad and red wine.
storage: Up to 3 days in the refrigerator. Reheat under a lid over low heat.
