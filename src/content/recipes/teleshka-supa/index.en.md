mise:
  - Rinse the meat with cold water.
  - Peel the carrots and the onion and leave them whole; wash the celery.
  - Peel the potatoes later so that they do not darken.
technique: The meat is covered with cold water and heated slowly – this way the proteins rise as foam, which is skimmed off, and the broth stays clear.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the meat, the carrots, the celery, the onion, the potatoes and the spices.
  - title: Boil the meat
    text: Cover the meat with the cold water and bring it to the boil over medium heat. Skim the foam until it stops appearing, then reduce the heat to the lowest setting. Add the black peppercorns and the bay leaf and cook for about an hour and a half.
    why: In cold water the proteins are released slowly and gather as foam that is easy to skim.
    avoid: Hard boiling breaks the foam and the fat into tiny droplets and the broth turns cloudy.
    time: 90
  - title: Add the vegetables
    text: Add the whole carrots, the celery and the onion to the meat and salt. Cook for another 20 minutes.
    time: 20
  - title: Add the potatoes
    text: Take out the meat and the vegetables and cut them into bite-sized pieces. Dice the potatoes and drop them into the broth together with the meat and the vegetables. Cook for 20 minutes.
    cue: The potatoes are soft and the meat comes apart with a spoon.
    time: 20
  - title: Season
    text: Taste for salt, sprinkle with parsley and ladle the soup into warmed bowls. Add a slice of lemon to each.
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve with fresh bread and black pepper at the table.
tips:
  - Meat with a bone or with a little gristle gives a richer broth.
  - If you want a completely clear broth, strain it through a fine sieve before returning the meat.
mistakes:
  - Boiling water at the start – the broth turns cloudy.
  - Salting right at the start – the broth reduces and may become over-salted.
substitutions:
  - Celeriac or parsnip instead of celery stalks.
  - A handful of vermicelli instead of potatoes, added in the last 5 minutes.
plating: "In a deep bowl: meat, vegetables and broth, with parsley and a slice of lemon on the rim."
serving: With fresh bread, lemon and freshly ground black pepper.
storage: Up to 3 days in the refrigerator. The fat skimmed off the cold broth makes it lighter.
