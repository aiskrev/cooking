mise:
  - Pat the quails dry, cut out the backbone and flatten them (spatchcock).
  - Clean the artichokes and put them in acidulated water so that they do not darken.
  - Cut the orange and lemon into segments.
technique: Spatchcocking for even cooking, searing with butter, basting with aromatics, a light sauce of citrus and jus.
temperature: Sear for 3–4 minutes on each side, then finish at 200 °C for 6–8 minutes until 72 °C in the thickest part. Rest for 5 minutes.
balance: The sweet poultry meat is balanced by the acidity of the citrus and the bitterness of the artichoke.
