  - Chill a tray for the granita in the freezer.
  - Whip the mascarpone, cream, sugar and vanilla seeds to a thick cream and refrigerate.
  - Heat the oven to 170 °C and line a tray with paper.
technique: A granita crystallised by regular scraping in the freezer; tuiles from a thinly spread batter; fruit caramelised in butter; spooned quenelles.
temperature: The granita freezes for 4 hours, scraped with a fork every 30–40 minutes. The tuiles bake at 170 °C for 6–8 minutes until golden. The peaches caramelise over a medium heat for 2–3 minutes on each side.
balance: The sweetness of the peaches and cream is balanced by the sharp, herbal granita. The tuiles bring a toasted almond note and crunch.

steps:
  - title: Lay out the ingredients
    text: Lay out the peaches, mascarpone, almonds, verbena, sugar and butter.
  - title: Make the granita
    text: Bring the water and sugar to the boil, take off the heat, add the verbena and leave for 20 minutes. Strain, add the lemon juice, pour into the cold tray and freeze for 4 hours, scraping with a fork every 30–40 minutes.
    cue: The crystals are fine, dry and greenish-gold.
    time: 240
  - title: Make the tuile batter
    text: Stir the melted butter, icing sugar, egg white and flour into a smooth batter. Spread in thin strips on the paper and sprinkle with the flaked almonds.
  - title: Bake the tuiles
    text: Bake at 170 °C for 6–8 minutes until golden and leave to cool on a rack.
    time: 8
  - title: Slice the peaches
    text: Cut the peaches into thick wedges around the stone.
  - title: Caramelise the peaches
    text: Melt the butter with the sugar in a pan and caramelise the wedges for 2–3 minutes on each side. Add a few verbena leaves for aroma.
    avoid: Do not stir often – the peaches need to sit still to colour.
    time: 6
  - title: Plate
    text: Place the warm peaches on the plate, shape 2–3 quenelles of cream with two spoons, add the granita at the last moment and stand the tuiles upright. Decorate with flowers.
tips:
  - If you have no lemon verbena, use lemon balm, or lemon zest with mint.
  - The tuiles stay crisp in a tin for up to 3 days.
  - Scrape the granita again just before serving.
mistakes:
  - Batter spread too thick – the tuiles turn soft.
  - Granita left unscraped – it becomes a block of ice.
  - Unripe peaches – they will not caramelise and taste sour.
substitutions:
  - Instead of peaches – nectarines or apricots.
  - Instead of mascarpone – strained yoghurt with honey.
plating: "A wide pale plate: wedges of caramelised peach, quenelles of cream, granita on top and upright almond tuiles."
serving: "At once – the granita melts quickly. Suitable non-alcoholic pairings: cold verbena tea or peach nectar with sparkling water."
storage: The granita keeps in the freezer for up to 1 week, the cream in the refrigerator for up to 2 days. Caramelise the peaches just before serving.
