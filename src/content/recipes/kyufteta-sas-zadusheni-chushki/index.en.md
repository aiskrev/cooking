mise:
  - Grate the onion for the kyufteta on a fine grater.
  - Cut the remaining onion and the peppers into cubes of about 1 cm and chop the tomatoes finely.
  - Wet your hands before shaping the kyufteta.
technique: The kyufteta are sealed in a frying pan and finished under a lid on top of the vegetables. The juice they release and that of the vegetables form a light sauce.
temperature: Sear over medium to high heat, then 20–25 minutes over low heat under a lid. The kyufteta must be cooked all the way through – 71 °C in the middle.
balance: The kyufteta are dense and spiced with cumin, the vegetables sweet and slightly sour. A spoonful of tomato purée thickens and rounds out the sauce.

steps:
  - title: Mix the kyufteta
    text: Knead the mince with the grated onion, the egg, the breadcrumbs, the salt, the black pepper and the cumin to a uniform mixture. Shape into balls of about 60 g.
    cue: The mince is sticky and comes together in one mass.
  - title: Sear the kyufteta
    text: Heat 2 tbsp of the oil in a frying pan over medium to high heat and sear the kyufteta for 2–3 minutes on each side, in two batches. Take them out.
    why: Seared kyufteta hold their shape and release flavour into the sauce.
    avoid: Do not move them before a crust has formed – they stick.
  - title: Braise the vegetables
    text: In the same fat, with the remaining oil, sweat the onion and the peppers for 6–8 minutes. Add the tomatoes, the tomato purée, the hot water and, off the heat, the paprika.
    time: 8
  - title: Return the kyufteta
    text: Arrange the kyufteta on the vegetables, cover and let them simmer for 20–25 minutes over low heat.
    cue: The sauce is thickened and the vegetables are soft.
    time: 25
  - title: Serve
    text: Sprinkle with parsley and serve in a clay bowl with fresh bread and hot peppers.
tips:
  - Wet hands help with the shaping.
  - Leave the mince for 30 minutes in the refrigerator – the kyufteta hold their shape better.
mistakes:
  - An overcrowded pan – the kyufteta boil instead of searing.
  - High heat under the lid – the vegetables burn.
substitutions:
  - Instead of breadcrumbs – a slice of dry bread, soaked and squeezed out.
  - A splash of wine for a richer sauce.
plating: The kyufteta in a deep clay bowl on top of the vegetables, with parsley on top.
serving: With fresh bread, hot peppers and a glass of red wine.
storage: Up to 3 days in the refrigerator; they are also suitable for freezing.
