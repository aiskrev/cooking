mise:
  - Scrub the carrots with a brush without peeling them; leave 2 cm of the stems.
  - Toast the pistachios in a dry pan and chop them roughly.
  - If the sea buckthorn is frozen, thaw it in the refrigerator.
technique: Whole carrots roasted at a high temperature; a blanched and blended herb oil; seasoned strained yoghurt.
temperature: The carrots roast at 220 °C for 25–30 minutes, turned once. The herbs are blanched for 10 seconds and the oil is blended warm (up to 60 °C) so it stays green.
balance: The sweetness of roast carrots and honey is balanced by the yoghurt and the very sour sea buckthorn; pistachios and cumin add depth.

steps:
  - title: Roast the carrots
    text: Toss the carrots with olive oil, honey, salt and cumin and roast at 220 °C for 25–30 minutes, until tender and dark at the edges.
    cue: A knife goes in easily and the edges are dark brown.
    time: 30
  - title: Make the herb oil
    text: Blanch the parsley and dill for 10 seconds, cool them in iced water, squeeze dry and blend with the warm oil for 2 minutes. Strain through muslin.
  - title: Season the yoghurt
    text: Stir the strained yoghurt with the grated garlic, lemon juice and salt.
  - title: Plate
    text: Spread the yoghurt with the back of a spoon. Arrange the carrots on top, scatter the sea buckthorn and pistachios, add drops of herb oil and finish with dill and the carrot tops.
tips:
  - Sea buckthorn is very sour – start with a little and add to taste.
  - Carrots of different colours look even better.
  - The herb oil keeps in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
mistakes:
  - Carrots crowded in the tray – they steam instead of roasting.
  - Herbs blanched too long – the oil turns brownish.
  - Hot carrots served late on cold yoghurt – the yoghurt thins out.
substitutions:
  - Instead of sea buckthorn – pomegranate seeds or sour cherries.
  - Instead of strained yoghurt – labneh, or curd cheese whipped with cream.
plating: "A wide pale plate: yoghurt as the base, the carrots piled up, sea buckthorn and pistachios, green drops of oil and dill."
serving: "Warm or at room temperature, as a starter or a side for lamb. Suitable non-alcoholic pairing: carrot and orange juice with ginger."
storage: The carrots keep in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; keep the yoghurt and oil separately.
