mise:
  - Mix the miso with the sake, mirin and sugar into a smooth marinade paste.
  - Coat the fish with the marinade and leave it in the refrigerator for 24 hours, covered.
  - Cut the daikon into thick rounds and peel them.
technique: Marinating in miso for 24 hours, roasting until caramelised, simmering daikon in dashi, a citrus finish.
temperature: "Oven at 200 °C (180 °C fan) or grill: 8–10 minutes, until the crust darkens. Core 52–55 °C."
balance: Miso is sweet and salty, yuzu acidic and aromatic, daikon clean and neutral.

tips:
  - Miso burns easily – wipe off the excess before roasting.
  - The marinating time can be 12–48 hours.
mistakes:
  - Too much miso left on during roasting – it burns.
  - Too much yuzu zest.
substitutions:
  - Instead of yuzu – a mixture of lemon and mandarin.
  - Instead of black cod – salmon or cod.
plating: "A dark deep bowl: daikon at the bottom, fish on top, a little broth, yuzu and shiso."
serving: With white rice and a glass of sake or Riesling.
storage: The fish keeps marinated for up to 2 days; the finished dish is best straight away.
