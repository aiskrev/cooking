mise:
  - Cut the brioche into cubes and toast it in the oven until golden.
  - Clean the porcini with a brush and slice them.
  - Have kitchen string and a board ready.
technique: Butterflying and flattening turkey breast; a mushroom and brioche stuffing; rolling and tying a roulade; searing and finishing in the oven; a pan gravy with a reduction.
temperature: The roulade is seared on all sides for 6–8 minutes and roasted at 170 °C for about 45–55 minutes – to 68 °C in the centre. It rests for 15 minutes, during which it reaches 72 °C.
balance: Lean turkey gets juiciness from the butter and stuffing; porcini add depth, cranberries acidity, sage aroma.

steps:
  - title: Lay out the ingredients
    text: Lay out the turkey breast, porcini, cranberries, brioche, shallots, herbs and butter.
    photo: 01-pueshko-stapka-1.webp
  - title: Prepare the mise en place
    text: Chop the shallots and sage, get the toasted brioche ready and measure out the stock and wine.
    photo: 02-pueshko-stapka-2.webp
  - title: Make the stuffing
    text: Soften the shallots in the butter, add the porcini and sauté for 5–6 minutes. Separately, cook the cranberries with the sugar and juice for 5 minutes. Mix the mushrooms with the brioche, 80 g of raw cranberries, the sage, parsley and egg, and season with salt.
    photo: 03-pueshko-stapka-3.webp
  - title: Butterfly and fill
    text: Cut the breast horizontally and open it like a book. Flatten it between two sheets of film to 1.5 cm thick, season and spread the stuffing, leaving 2 cm free at the edges.
    photo: 04-pueshko-stapka-4.webp
  - title: Roll
    text: Roll up tightly from the long side, holding the stuffing in place.
    photo: 05-pueshko-stapka-5.webp
  - title: Tie
    text: Tie the roulade with string every 2–3 cm so it keeps its shape. Season the outside with salt and pepper.
    photo: 06-pueshko-stapka-6.webp
  - title: Sear and baste
    text: Sear the roulade in the oil on all sides for 6–8 minutes. Add the butter, thyme and garlic and baste for 2 minutes.
    photo: 07-pueshko-stapka-7.webp
  - title: Roast
    text: Transfer to the oven at 170 °C and roast for 45–55 minutes, basting every 15 minutes. Rest for 15 minutes.
    cue: The thermometer reads 68 °C in the centre; the juices run clear.
    avoid: Do not slice at once – the stuffing will fall apart.
    time: 50
    photo: 08-pueshko-stapka-8.webp
  - title: Make the gravy
    text: Pour off the excess fat, soften the shallots in the pan, deglaze with the wine and add the stock. Simmer for 10 minutes until thick, strain and stir in the cold butter.
    photo: 09-pueshko-stapka-9.webp
  - title: Serve
    text: Remove the string, cut the roulade into 2 cm slices and serve with the gravy and the cranberry compote.
    photo: 10-pueshko-stapka-10.webp
tips:
  - Out of season, use dried porcini soaked for 20 minutes, with button mushrooms.
  - The roulade can be stuffed and tied a day ahead and kept in the refrigerator.
  - Cold slices make excellent sandwiches.
mistakes:
  - Unevenly flattened breast – the thin parts dry out.
  - Too much stuffing – the roulade will not close and falls apart.
  - Roasting without a thermometer – turkey dries out easily.
substitutions:
  - Instead of turkey – chicken breast (a smaller roulade, shorter roasting).
  - Instead of brioche – white bread.
  - Instead of cranberries – dried cherries.
plating: "A large platter or individual plates: slices of roulade with the stuffing visible, a spoonful of gravy and a small bowl of cranberries."
serving: "Warm, for a festive table, with mashed potatoes or roast vegetables. Suitable non-alcoholic pairings: apple juice with cinnamon, or cranberry juice with sparkling water."
storage: The roasted roulade keeps in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat it sliced, covered, with gravy.
