mise:
  - Chop 1 kg of tomatoes with the salt and basil, put them in muslin over a bowl and leave to drain in the refrigerator for 8 hours.
  - Slice the tomatoes and halve the strawberries.
  - Beat the cheese with a little olive oil until creamy.
technique: Draining tomato water through muslin, seasoning raw tomatoes, cutting different varieties.
temperature: Everything is served cold or at room temperature; the tomato water is chilled.
balance: The acidity of the tomatoes is softened by the sweetness of the strawberries; the cheese adds fat.
