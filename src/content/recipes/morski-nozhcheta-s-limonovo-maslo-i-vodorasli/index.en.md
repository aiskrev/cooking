mise:
  - Soak the razor clams for 1 hour in cold salted water (20 g salt per litre) so they release their sand.
  - If the seaweed is dried, soak it for 10 minutes and chop it.
  - Char the lemon halves cut-side down in a dry pan until dark.
technique: Steaming shellfish open, cleaning the meat, a butter sauce from the cooking liquor (beurre blanc), quick assembly in the shell.
temperature: The clams cook over high heat under a lid for 60–90 seconds – just until they open. The sauce is reduced over high heat, then the butter is whisked in over low heat without boiling.
balance: "The sweet meat needs only acidity and salinity: lemon gives freshness, seaweed iodine, butter richness. Hardly any salt is needed."

steps:
  - title: Lay out the ingredients
    text: Arrange the soaked razor clams, lemon, garlic, butter, wine, seaweed and herbs.
    photo: 01-morski-stapka-1.webp
  - title: Steam open
    text: Heat a wide pot, add the clams, wine, garlic and thyme and cover at once. Cook for 60–90 seconds, lifting out the clams as soon as they open. Strain and keep the liquor.
    cue: The shells are open and the meat is just opaque.
    avoid: Discard any clams that do not open.
    time: 2
  - title: Clean the meat
    text: Take the meat out of the shells. Remove the dark sac (the stomach) and discard it. Cut the meat into 1 cm pieces. Wash the shells.
    photo: 02-morski-stapka-3.webp
  - title: Make the lemon butter
    text: Sweat the shallot and garlic in a little butter, add 100 ml of the strained liquor and reduce by half. Over low heat whisk in the diced butter, then add the lemon juice and zest.
    cue: The sauce is creamy, glossy and does not split.
    time: 8
  - title: Assemble
    text: Mix the sliced meat with half the sauce and the herbs. Spoon it into the shells, add the seaweed and pour over the rest of the sauce.
    photo: 03-morski-stapka-5.webp
  - title: Serve
    text: Arrange the shells on a large plate, garnish with lemon zest, small flowers and the charred lemon and add a drop of olive oil.
    photo: 04-morski-stapka-6.webp
tips:
  - Live razor clams retract when touched – a sign of freshness.
  - Cook in batches so the first ones to open do not overcook.
mistakes:
  - Overcooked clams – they turn rubbery in seconds.
  - Sand left in – soaking is essential.
substitutions:
  - Instead of razor clams – mussels or carpet-shell clams.
  - Instead of seaweed – sea fennel or wild garlic.
plating: "A large flat plate: the shells in a row, the meat with seaweed and sauce in them, charred lemon beside."
serving: As a starter, with crusty bread and a dry white wine (Albariño, Sauvignon Blanc).
storage: Serve straight away; they do not keep.
allergens:

  - Milk and dairy
  - Sulphites (wine)
