mise:
  - Peel the artichokes down to the tender leaves, cut off the tips and put them in acidulated water.
  - Cut the lemon into rounds.
  - Toast the almonds dry.
technique: Braising in liquid, roasting, a purée, a green herb sauce.
temperature: Braise over low heat for 25–30 minutes; roast at 220 °C for 10–15 minutes.
balance: The bitterness of the artichoke is balanced by the butter and lemon.
