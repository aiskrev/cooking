mise:
  - Take the meat out of the refrigerator 30 minutes ahead and dry it.
  - Finely grate only the yellow part of the zest and mix it with the salt.
technique: Dry searing in a very hot heavy pan – the fat of the meat melts and the meat cooks in it. A rest follows, and slicing across the grain.
temperature: 1.5–2 minutes on each side over high heat. For medium rare – 52 °C at the core when taken out.
balance: The meat is very rich and sweet; the salt and the aroma of the citrus zest are the only counterpoint. The portion is small – 150 g per person is enough.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the meat, the salt and the citrus.
  - title: Prepare the meat
    text: If the piece is large, divide it into portions about 2.5 cm thick. Dry it and leave it at room temperature.
    why: Cold meat cooks unevenly – done on the outside, icy inside.
  - title: Sear
    text: Heat a heavy pan, with no fat, until it begins to smoke. Put in the meat and cook it for 1.5–2 minutes on each side, turning it only once.
    cue: The crust is dark brown, and a pink stripe can be seen at the side.
    avoid: Do not add oil – the fat of the meat melts within seconds.
    time: 4
  - title: Let it rest
    text: Move the meat onto a board and leave it for 5 minutes.
    why: The juice returns into the fibres and stays in the meat, not on the board.
    time: 5
  - title: Slice and salt
    text: Slice the meat across the grain into slices about half a centimetre thick and sprinkle it with the citrus salt and a pinch of black pepper.
  - title: Serve
    text: Arrange the slices on a warmed plate and serve immediately.
tips:
  - Salt after cooking – the coarse flakes stay crisp and are tasted separately.
  - The fat rendered in the pan is excellent for frying rice or mushrooms.
mistakes:
  - A pan that is not hot enough – the fat runs out before a crust forms.
  - Cooking for too long – expensive meat loses the tenderness for which it is expensive.
substitutions:
  - Well-aged sirloin from a local breed, with a drop of oil in the pan.
  - Lime or orange zest instead of lemon.
plating: The slices, slightly overlapping, in one line on a dark plate; a little citrus salt and a slice of lemon on the side.
serving: With a light green salad or steamed rice.
storage: Eaten immediately.
