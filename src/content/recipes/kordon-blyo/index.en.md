steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the fillets, the ham, the kashkaval, the flour, the eggs and the breadcrumbs.
  - title: Pound the fillets
    text: Put each fillet between two sheets of cling film and pound it with the smooth side of a mallet or with a rolling pin to a thickness of about half a centimetre. Season with salt and sprinkle with black pepper.
    why: An even thickness means even cooking and easy rolling.
    avoid: Do not strike hard – the meat tears and the cheese will leak out.
  - title: Roll
    text: Lay two slices of ham and some kashkaval on each fillet, leaving a finger's width free along the edge. Fold in the sides and roll into a tight roll. Wrap each one in cling film and leave it for 30 minutes in the refrigerator.
    why: Cold, the roll firms up and does not unroll during breading.
  - title: Bread
    text: Roll the rolls in the flour, then in the eggs and finally in the breadcrumbs, pressing. For a secure crust, repeat the egg and the breadcrumbs once more.
  - title: Brown and finish
    text: Heat the butter with the oil in a frying pan over medium heat and brown the rolls on all sides for 5–6 minutes. Transfer them to the tray and finish them for 15 minutes in the oven.
    cue: The coating is evenly golden, and a thermometer in the middle shows 74 °C.
    time: 15
  - title: Make the sauce and slice
    text: In the pan used for browning, sweat the shallot for 2 minutes, pour in the cream and let it simmer for 3–4 minutes. Stir in the mustard and season with salt. Let the rolls rest for 5 minutes and slice them into thick rounds.
  - title: Serve
    text: Spread the sauce on warmed plates and arrange the rounds on it, cut side up.
tips:
  - If the roll will not stay closed, secure it with a toothpick and remove it before slicing.
  - The kashkaval must not reach the edge – if it leaks into the pan, it burns.
mistakes:
  - Unevenly pounded fillet – thin spots dry out, thick ones stay raw.
  - Slicing at once – the cheese runs out onto the board.
substitutions:
  - Emmental or gruyère instead of kashkaval; prosciutto instead of ham.
  - "Entirely in the oven, without browning: 25–30 minutes at 200 °C, sprayed with oil."
plating: Three or four rounds, slightly overlapping, on the cream sauce; a few leaves of greenery on top.
serving: With mashed potatoes or a green salad with lemon.
storage: Up to 2 days in the refrigerator. Reheat in the oven so that the coating stays crisp.
