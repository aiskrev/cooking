mise:
  - Cut the butter for the sauce into cubes and return it to the refrigerator.
  - Chop the shallot as finely as possible; crush the garlic.
  - Warm the plates.
technique: The wine is reduced with the shallot almost to dryness, and then cold butter is whisked into it, piece by piece. The prawns are seared in a very hot pan in a single layer.
temperature: The sauce is kept warm, but not hot – above about 60 °C it separates. The prawns are cooked for 1.5–2 minutes on each side.
balance: The prawns are sweet, and the sauce is buttery and sharp at the same time. If it lacks sharpness, add a few drops of lemon.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the prawns, the butter, the shallot, the wine, the garlic and the lemon.
    photo: 01-skaridi-stapka-1.webp
  - title: Clean the prawns
    text: Peel the prawns, leaving the tails on. Cut shallowly along the back and remove the dark vein. Rinse them and dry them very well.
    why: The vein is the prawn's intestine and can be gritty and bitter.
    photo: 02-skaridi-stapka-2.webp
  - title: Make the sauce
    text: Boil the shallot with the wine and the lemon juice in a small saucepan until about one tablespoon of liquid remains. Lower the heat to the lowest setting and add the cold butter cube by cube, whisking constantly. Season with salt and keep the sauce in a warm place, not on the hob.
    cue: The sauce is pale, as thick as pouring cream and glossy.
    avoid: If the sauce boils or the butter goes in all at once, the emulsion separates into fat and liquid.
  - title: Sear the prawns
    text: Heat the oil in a wide frying pan over high heat. Season the prawns with salt and arrange them in a single layer. Cook them for 1.5–2 minutes, turn them over, add the butter and the garlic and cook for another 1–2 minutes.
    cue: The prawns are pink, opaque and curved like the letter C.
    avoid: Prawns curled into a tight circle are overcooked and rubbery.
    time: 4
    photo: 03-skaridi-stapka-4.webp
  - title: Spoon over the sauce
    text: Take the pan off the heat, remove the garlic and spoon a few spoonfuls of the sauce over the prawns.
    photo: 04-skaridi-stapka-5.webp
  - title: Serve
    text: Pour the remaining sauce onto the warmed plates, arrange the prawns on it and serve immediately.
    photo: 05-skaridi-stapka-6.webp
tips:
  - A spoonful of cream added to the reduction before the butter makes the sauce much more stable.
  - The shells and the heads make a quick stock – do not throw them away.
mistakes:
  - Wet prawns – they release water and boil.
  - A sauce left on the hob – it separates within minutes.
substitutions:
  - Frozen peeled prawns, thawed in the refrigerator and dried well.
  - Tarragon or chives in the finished sauce.
plating: The prawns in a circle on the sauce, tails outwards, and a few sprigs of greenery in the middle.
serving: With toasted bread to mop up the sauce, and the same white wine.
storage: Eaten immediately. The butter sauce is not stored and not reheated.
allergens:
