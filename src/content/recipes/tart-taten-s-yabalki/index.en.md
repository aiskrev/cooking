mise:
  - Peel the apples, cut them into quarters and remove the cores.
  - Choose a frying pan 24 cm in diameter that can go into the oven.
  - Switch the oven on to 200 °C.
technique: The sugar is melted dry in the pan to amber, the butter is added and the apples are arranged tightly. The pastry goes on top and is tucked in around the edge.
temperature: Oven at 200 °C (180 °C fan), 30–35 minutes. The caramel is about 170 °C – do not touch it and do not taste it.
balance: The caramel is sweet and slightly bitter, the apples sharp and soft. The cold vanilla ice cream on top balances the warm tart.

steps:
  - title: Make the caramel
    text: Melt the sugar in the pan over medium heat, without stirring, until amber. Off the heat add the butter and the lemon juice and stir carefully.
    cue: The caramel is the colour of dark honey.
    avoid: The caramel is extremely hot – do not touch it and keep your face to one side.
  - title: Prepare the apples
    text: Arrange the quarters, rounded side down, in a circle, tightly, in the caramel. Cook over medium heat for 10 minutes until the caramel bubbles and the apples soften slightly.
    why: Pre-cooking reduces the moisture that would otherwise make the tart watery.
    time: 10
  - title: Cover with pastry
    text: Roll the puff pastry into a circle slightly larger than the pan. Cover the apples and tuck the edges inwards. Prick in several places.
  - title: Bake
    text: Bake for 30–35 minutes at 200 °C, until the pastry has puffed up and is dark golden.
    time: 35
  - title: Turn out
    text: Leave it for 5 minutes. Run a knife around the edge, cover with a plate and turn it over in one movement. Rearrange the apples if they have moved.
    avoid: Do not wait longer than 10 minutes – the caramel sets and sticks.
    time: 5
  - title: Serve
    text: Cut into pieces and serve warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
tips:
  - Apples that do not fall apart (such as Golden Delicious or Granny Smith) are best.
  - The tart is ready when the caramel bubbles around the edge of the pan.
mistakes:
  - Pale caramel – the tart is bland.
  - Bitter caramel – it is burnt, start again.
substitutions:
  - Pears instead of apples.
  - A pinch of cinnamon or a few cloves in the caramel.
plating: A piece of tart with glossy apples, a scoop of ice cream beside it, drops of caramel and a mint leaf.
serving: Warm, with cold vanilla ice cream.
storage: Best on the day it is made; up to 2 days in the refrigerator, reheated in the oven.
