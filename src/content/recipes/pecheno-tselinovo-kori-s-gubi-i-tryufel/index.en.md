mise:
  - Peel the celeriac, keeping its shape.
  - Clean the mushrooms with a brush and slice them.
  - Crush the hazelnuts.
technique: Roasting a whole root with butter and thyme, searing mushrooms, a mushroom sauce.
temperature: Oven at 170 °C (150 °C fan) for 90–120 minutes, basting from time to time; the last 15 minutes at 200 °C for a crust. It is ready when a knife goes in without resistance.
balance: The celeriac is sweet, the mushrooms earthy, the truffle aromatic, and the soy sauce adds umami.
