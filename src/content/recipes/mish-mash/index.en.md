mise:
  - Peel the tomatoes and cut them into cubes.
  - Finely chop the onion and crumble the cheese.
technique: The peppers are roasted over an open flame for their smoky flavour, and the tomatoes are cooked down before the eggs go in – otherwise the mish-mash turns watery.
temperature: The sauce is thickened over medium heat. The eggs go in on the lowest heat and are taken off while they are still glossy.
balance: The sweetness of the roasted peppers, the acidity of the tomatoes and the salt of the cheese. Salt only at the end and after tasting.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the peppers, the tomatoes, the onion, the eggs, the cheese and the butter.
  - title: Roast the peppers
    text: Roast the peppers over an open flame, on a grill or in a pepper roaster (chushkopek) until their skin is blackened and cracked.
    time: 10
  - title: Turn them
    text: Turn the peppers with tongs so that they roast evenly on all sides.
    cue: The skin is blistered black all over and the flesh is soft.
  - title: Peel and cut
    text: Put the peppers in a covered pot for 10 minutes to steam. Peel them, remove the seeds and cut them into strips.
    why: The steam under the lid loosens the skin and it peels off in one movement.
    time: 10
  - title: Sweat the onion
    text: Melt the butter in a heavy frying pan and sweat the onion over medium heat until soft and lightly coloured.
    time: 8
  - title: Thicken the sauce
    text: Add the tomatoes, the peppers and the paprika and cook for about 10 minutes, stirring.
    cue: The liquid has evaporated and the spoon leaves a trail on the bottom.
    time: 10
  - title: Add the cheese
    text: Lower the heat to the lowest setting and sprinkle the crumbled cheese over the sauce.
  - title: Add the eggs and serve
    text: Break the eggs into the pan and stir them gently two or three times, with broad movements. After 1–2 minutes take it off the heat, sprinkle with parsley and serve immediately.
    cue: The eggs are set but still glossy.
    avoid: Vigorous stirring over high heat makes the eggs dry and crumbly.
    time: 2
tips:
  - The cheese is salty – taste before salting.
  - Roasted peppers from the freezer or from a jar save half the work.
mistakes:
  - A watery sauce – the eggs do not set and the dish turns mushy.
  - High heat with the eggs – they turn rubbery.
substitutions:
  - Green peppers instead of red – the flavour is sharper and less sweet.
  - One hot pepper, if you like it spicy.
plating: Straight in the pan, placed on a wooden board in the middle of the table.
serving: With fresh or toasted bread.
storage: Eaten immediately; reheated, it loses the softness of the eggs.
allergens:
  - Eggs
  - Milk and dairy
