mise:
  - Remove the heads and tails of the langoustines; keep the shells for the sauce.
  - Cut the fennel into thin strips.
  - Cut the orange into segments with no membrane.
technique: Roasting shells for a sauce base, simmering with cognac, quickly searing the tails, fennel with orange.
temperature: The tails cook for 1.5–2 minutes on each side; no more – they easily turn rubbery. The sauce simmers for 20 minutes.
balance: The sweet meat and sweet sauce are balanced by the fennel and orange.

tips:
  - Do not overcook the tails.
  - The shells give the colour and aroma of the sauce.
mistakes:

  - Letting the sauce boil after the butter.
