mise:
  - Take the meat out of the refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking.
  - Crush the cocoa nibs and peppercorns coarsely in a mortar.
  - Finely chop the shallots and halve the endives.
technique: Trimming the silverskin from a loin; a cocoa and pepper crust; searing and butter basting; resting; caramelised endive; a wine and stock reduction with cherries.
temperature: The loin is seared over a high heat for 2 minutes on each side and basted with butter for another 3–4 minutes – to 52–54 °C in the centre. It rests for 8 minutes. The endive caramelises over a medium heat for 6–8 minutes.
balance: The bitterness of cocoa and endive is balanced by the sour-sweet cherry sauce. The lean meat gets richness from the butter.

  - title: Trim and season
    text: Remove the silverskin with a thin knife. Season the meat with salt and roll it in the cocoa and pepper mixture.

  - title: Sear
    text: Heat the oil in a pan until it just smokes and sear the meat for 2 minutes on each side.
    photo: 04-elensko-stapka-4.webp
  - title: Baste with butter
    text: Lower the heat, add the butter, thyme and garlic and baste the meat for 3–4 minutes. Rest for 8 minutes.
    cue: The thermometer reads 52–54 °C in the thickest part.
    avoid: Venison is very lean – above 58 °C it turns dry.

tips:
  - If there is no venison, use roe deer loin or beef fillet.
  - Cocoa nibs are sold in health food shops.
  - The sauce can be made a day ahead.
mistakes:
  - Meat put into the pan cold – it sears unevenly.
  - Slicing straight after cooking – it loses its juices.
  - Sugar in the sauce – the cherries are sweet enough.
substitutions:
  - Instead of cherries – sour cherries or blackberries.
  - Instead of endive – radicchio, seared the same way.
plating: "A dark plate: slices of pink meat with a crust, two endive halves, a glossy sauce and whole cherries."
serving: "Warm, as a main course. Suitable non-alcoholic pairings: cherry juice with sparkling water or rosehip tea."
storage: The meat is best at once. The sauce keeps in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
