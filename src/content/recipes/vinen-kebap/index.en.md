mise:
  - Cut the meat into cubes of about 3 cm and pat it dry with kitchen paper.
  - Cut the onion and the carrots into small dice.
  - Wash the rice and leave it to drain; chop the parsley finely.
technique: Searing the meat, sweating the vegetables in the same pot, reducing the wine and long simmering under a lid until the sauce thickens by itself.
temperature: Simmering on the lowest heat for 80–90 minutes – an occasional bubble appears on the surface, but it does not boil.
balance: The wine and the tomato purée provide acidity, the onion and the carrot sweetness. If the sauce is too acidic, a pinch of sugar sorts it out.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the meat, the vegetables, the wine, the stock and the spices.
    photo: 01-vinen-stapka-1.webp
  - title: Cut the meat
    text: Cut the shoulder into cubes of 3 cm, dry them and salt them.
    photo: 02-vinen-stapka-2.webp
  - title: Sear the meat
    text: Heat the oil in a heavy pot over high heat. Sear the meat in two batches, about 4 minutes each, and take it out.
    cue: Each piece has a brown crust on at least two sides.
    avoid: Do not stir constantly – meat browns when it stays in one place.
    time: 8
    photo: 03-vinen-stapka-3.webp
  - title: Sweat the onion and the carrot
    text: Lower the heat, add the butter, the onion and the carrots and cook for 6–8 minutes. Add the tomato purée and fry it for 1 minute, then stir in the paprika for a few seconds.
    why: Fried tomato purée loses its raw flavour and darkens.
    time: 8
    photo: 04-vinen-stapka-4.webp
  - title: Pour in the wine
    text: Pour in the wine and scrape the bottom with a wooden spoon. Let it boil for about 5 minutes.
    why: The alcohol evaporates and the full acidity of the wine remains.
    cue: The wine has reduced by half and begins to thicken.
    time: 5
    photo: 05-vinen-stapka-5.webp
  - title: Let it simmer
    text: Return the meat to the pot, add the stock, the bay leaves and the black peppercorns. Cover, leaving the lid slightly ajar, and let it simmer for 80–90 minutes on the lowest heat.
    cue: The meat comes apart with a spoon, and the sauce coats the back of it.
    avoid: Hard boiling tightens the meat and it turns tough.
    time: 85
    photo: 06-vinen-stapka-6.webp
  - title: Cook the rice
    text: Twenty minutes before the end, melt half of the butter in a small pot, fry the rice for 1–2 minutes, pour in the hot water and add salt. Cover and cook for 15 minutes over low heat. Leave for 5 minutes under the lid, then stir in the remaining butter and the parsley.
    cue: The grains are soft, separate and have absorbed all the water.
    time: 20
    photo: 07-vinen-stapka-7.webp
  - title: Serve
    text: Remove the bay leaves, taste the sauce for salt and serve the meat over the rice with plenty of sauce.
    photo: 08-vinen-stapka-8.webp
tips:
  - Cook with a wine that you would drink – the flaws of a bad wine are amplified in reduction.
  - If the sauce is thin at the end, take off the lid for the last 10 minutes.
mistakes:
  - Boiling instead of simmering – the meat turns dry and tough.
  - Paprika in very hot fat – it burns and the sauce turns bitter.
substitutions:
  - Beef shoulder instead of pork – cook for 30–40 minutes longer.
  - The rice can be replaced with mashed potatoes.
plating: Rice shaped into a small bowl in the middle of the plate, the meat around it and sauce on top; a pinch of parsley.
serving: With a glass of the same red wine and a roasted pepper salad.
storage: Up to 3 days in the refrigerator. When reheated the sauce thickens – thin it with a few spoonfuls of water.
allergens:
  - Milk and dairy
  - Sulphites (wine)
