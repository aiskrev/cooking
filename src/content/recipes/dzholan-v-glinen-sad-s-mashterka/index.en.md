  - Take the knuckle out of the refrigerator 30 minutes beforehand.

balance: Glossy crisp skin, tender meat that falls from the bone, and soft onion with a sweet, rich sauce.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the knuckle
    text: Wash the knuckle, dry it very thoroughly and score the skin with a sharp knife into small diamonds, without cutting into the meat.
    why: Dry, scored skin becomes crisp and renders its fat.
  - title: Season
    text: Rub the knuckle with the salt, the black pepper, the paprika, the oil and crushed garlic. Leave it for 1 hour at room temperature.
    time: 60
  - title: Arrange in the pot
    text: In the clay pot, arrange the onion, the thyme and the remaining garlic, put in the knuckle and pour in the wine or water.
  - title: Roast covered
    text: Cover the pot and put it in a cold oven. Switch it on to 170 °C and roast for about 2 hours.
    cue: The meat comes apart easily when you pierce it with a fork.
    time: 120
  - title: Form the crust
    text: Uncover the pot, raise the temperature to 200 °C and roast for another 25–30 minutes, basting with the sauce.
    time: 30
  - title: Rest
    text: Leave the knuckle for 10 minutes outside the oven before serving.
    time: 10
tips:
  - Heat the clay pot together with the oven so that it does not crack.
  - The sauce from the pot can be strained and served separately.
mistakes:
  - Wet skin – it does not become crisp.
  - Putting an unglazed pot into an already hot oven – it may crack.
substitutions:
  - Beer instead of wine.
  - Potatoes or sauerkraut in the same pot.
plating: The whole knuckle in the pot, the sauce on the side, a sprig of thyme on top.
serving: With mashed or roasted potatoes and sauerkraut.
storage: Up to 3 days in the refrigerator; reheat covered in the oven.
