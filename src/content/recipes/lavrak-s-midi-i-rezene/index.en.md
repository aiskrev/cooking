mise:
  - Pat the fillets dry and leave them at room temperature for 10 minutes.
  - Wash the mussels and discard any that are open.
  - Cut the fennel into thin strips.
technique: Searing skin-on fish, opening mussels in wine, beurre blanc.
temperature: The fish – 3–4 minutes on the skin side and 1–2 minutes on the other; the core 52–54 °C. The mussels open in 3–4 minutes.
balance: The butter in the sauce is balanced by the vinegar and lemon, and the mussels add saltiness.
