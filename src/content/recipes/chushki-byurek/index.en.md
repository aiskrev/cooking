mise:
  - Choose straight peppers with firm walls – they are easier to stuff.
  - "Prepare three shallow plates: one with the flour, one with the beaten eggs and one with the breadcrumbs."
technique: The peppers are roasted and peeled without tearing them, stuffed not quite to the top and breaded in three stages – flour, egg, breadcrumbs.
temperature: "The oil is at about 175 °C: a pinch of breadcrumbs sizzles at once. Frying takes 2–3 minutes on each side."
balance: The sweet pepper and the salty cheese balance each other on their own; cold yogurt on the side freshens it.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the peppers, the cheese, the eggs, the parsley, the flour and the breadcrumbs.
  - title: Roast the peppers
    text: Roast the peppers over an open flame, in a pepper roaster (chushkopek) or under the oven grill, until the skin is black on all sides. Put them in a covered container for 10 minutes.
    time: 12
  - title: Peel them
    text: Peel the peppers carefully and take out the seeds through the opening at the stem, without tearing them. Pat them dry with kitchen paper.
    why: A whole pepper holds the filling; the coating sticks to a dry one.
  - title: Mix the filling
    text: Crumble the cheese with a fork and stir it together with the egg, the parsley and the black pepper.
  - title: Stuff the peppers
    text: Fill each pepper with a teaspoon, without overfilling it, and press it lightly so that it becomes flat and the opening closes.
    avoid: An overfilled pepper bursts in the oil and the filling leaks out.
  - title: Bread them
    text: Roll each pepper in the flour, then in the beaten eggs and finally in the breadcrumbs, pressing lightly.
    why: The flour dries the surface, the egg makes the crumbs stick, and the breadcrumbs give the crisp crust.
  - title: Fry
    text: Heat the oil in a wide frying pan and fry the peppers in two batches, 2–3 minutes on each side.
    cue: The coating is evenly golden.
    time: 6
  - title: Drain
    text: Lift the peppers onto a rack or kitchen paper.
    why: On a rack the underside stays crisp instead of steaming.
  - title: Check the filling
    text: Cut one pepper open – the filling should be hot and firm.
    cue: The cheese has softened and the egg in it is completely cooked.
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve the peppers warm, with a small bowl of cold yogurt.
tips:
  - Roasted peppers from the freezer work too, as long as they are whole and well dried.
  - For a thicker crust, repeat the egg and the breadcrumbs once more.
mistakes:
  - Wet peppers – the coating slides off.
  - Cold oil – the peppers absorb fat and turn soft.
substitutions:
  - Half of the cheese can be replaced with izvara (fresh curd cheese) or grated kashkaval.
  - "A lighter version: only flour and egg, without breadcrumbs."
plating: Two peppers, one cut on the diagonal, with a spoonful of yogurt and a sprig of parsley.
serving: Warm, with garlic yogurt or a tomato salad.
storage: Best straight away. Up to the next day in the refrigerator; reheat in the oven, not in the microwave.
