mise:
  - If the octopus is fresh, freeze it for 24 hours and thaw it slowly – the fibres soften.
  - Peel the potatoes and cut them into even pieces.
  - Chop the chorizo finely, almost to a mince.
technique: Slow-simmered octopus; grilling over embers with smoked paprika; mashed potato with butter; chorizo fried to crumbs and the red oil clarified; roasted and peeled peppers.
temperature: The octopus simmers at 90–95 °C for about 60–75 minutes, until a knife goes into the thickest part without effort. The tentacles are grilled over strong heat for 2–3 minutes on each side. The peppers roast at 230 °C for 25 minutes.
balance: Rich, spicy chorizo and salt are balanced by sweet roasted peppers and soft mash; sherry vinegar in the oil and fresh parsley bring acidity and freshness.

    text: Grill the tentacles and spring onions over strong embers or in a grill pan for 2–3 minutes on each side, until crisp and lightly charred at the edges.
    avoid: Do not grill for long – the cooked octopus only needs warming and charring.

tips:
  - Roasted peppers peel easily if they rest for 10 minutes in a covered bowl.
  - The octopus cooking liquid is an excellent stock for rice.
  - Chorizo is salty – season the mash with care.
mistakes:
  - A rolling boil – the octopus turns rubbery.
  - Wet tentacles on the grill – they will not char.
  - Chorizo fried over a high heat – it burns before it crisps.
substitutions:
  - Instead of chorizo – smoked ham or a paprika sausage.
  - Instead of mashed potato – white bean purée.

storage: The cooked octopus keeps in the refrigerator for up to 2 days; grill it just before serving.
