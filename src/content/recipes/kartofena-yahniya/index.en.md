mise:
  - Peel the potatoes and cut them into cubes about 3 cm long.
  - Finely chop the onion, slice the carrot into rounds and chop the tomatoes coarsely.
technique: The potatoes are first fried briefly in the fat with the onion so that they pick up flavour, and are then finished with a little liquid under a lid. The paprika goes in off the heat.
temperature: Low to medium heat, under a lid, 25–30 minutes.
balance: The potatoes are sweetish, the tomatoes sharp, and the savory and the paprika carry the aroma. The garlic at the end adds freshness.

steps:
  - title: Sweat the vegetables
    text: Heat the oil in a pot and sweat the onion and the carrot for 5 minutes over medium heat.
  - title: Fry the potatoes
    text: Add the potatoes and stir them for 3–4 minutes until they begin to take on colour.
  - title: Add the tomatoes and the spices
    text: Add the tomatoes, the savory and the salt and stir. Take the pot off the heat and sprinkle in the paprika.
    avoid: Paprika burns quickly – it goes in off the heat.
  - title: Cook
    text: Pour in the hot water, cover and let it simmer for 25–30 minutes over low heat.
    cue: The potatoes are tender but whole, and the sauce is lightly thickened.
    time: 30
  - title: Season and serve
    text: Take it off the heat, add the crushed garlic and the parsley and leave it for 5 minutes under the lid. Serve with bread.
    time: 5
tips:
  - Waxy potatoes keep their shape; floury ones thicken the sauce.
  - The garlic at the end is optional, but it adds a lot of aroma.
mistakes:
  - Too much water – the stew turns into a soup.
  - Paprika over the heat – the stew turns bitter.
substitutions:
  - A piece of pork fatback or smoked meat, fried at the start, for a non-lenten version.
  - Dried spearmint instead of savory.
plating: In a clay bowl, with parsley on top and garlic flatbread on the side.
serving: With toasted garlic flatbread, pickles or roasted peppers and yogurt with dill.
storage: Up to 3 days in the refrigerator. Reheat under a lid over low heat.
