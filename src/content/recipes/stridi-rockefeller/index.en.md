mise:
  - "Check the oysters: the shells must be tightly closed or close when tapped. Open ones that do not react are thrown away."
  - Scrub the shells under cold running water.
  - Switch the oven grill on to 230 °C and spread the coarse salt in a tray.
technique: The oyster is opened at the hinge between the two shells with a special knife and cut free from the shell. The spinach is sweated and squeezed dry so that it does not release water onto it.
temperature: Grill at 230 °C, on the top shelf, 5–7 minutes – until the crust is golden and the juice around the edge bubbles.
balance: The oyster is salty and mineral, the spinach and the butter soften it, and the Parmesan adds density. A few drops of lemon at the end are essential.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the oysters, the spinach, the shallots, the butter, the breadcrumbs, the Parmesan and the lemon.
  - title: Open the oysters
    text: Hold the oyster with a folded cloth, flat shell up. Push the tip of an oyster knife into the hinge at the pointed end, twist it until the shell gives and cut through the muscle under the top shell. Also cut the oyster free from underneath and leave it in the deep shell together with its liquor.
    avoid: Never point the knife towards your palm – the cloth is the protection of your hand.
  - title: Prepare the topping
    text: Sweat the finely chopped shallots and the garlic in the butter for 2 minutes, add the spinach and cook until wilted. Squeeze it dry, chop it finely and mix it with the parsley and black pepper.
  - title: Top the oysters
    text: Arrange the oysters on the coarse salt so that they stay upright. Put a spoonful of the topping on each and sprinkle with the breadcrumbs mixed with the Parmesan.
    why: The salt keeps the shells level and the liquor does not spill.
  - title: Grill
    text: Grill for 5–7 minutes.
    cue: The crust is golden and the juice around the edge of the shells is bubbling.
    avoid: A minute more and the oysters shrink and turn rubbery.
    time: 6
  - title: Serve
    text: Serve the oysters immediately, on the salt or on crushed ice, with slices of lemon.
tips:
  - If you have no experience of opening them, ask the fishmonger to open them for you and bake them within an hour or two.
  - A drop of aniseed rakia or mastika in the spinach comes close to the classic flavour.
mistakes:
  - Wet spinach – the topping floats in water.
  - Cooking for a long time – the oyster is ready as soon as it is warm.
substitutions:
  - Mussels in the shell, prepared in the same way, for a more accessible version.
  - Kashkaval instead of Parmesan.
plating: Six oysters in a circle on coarse salt or ice, with a slice of lemon in the middle.
serving: As a starter, with a well-chilled dry white wine.
storage: Eaten immediately; not stored and not reheated.
