mise:
  - Peel the asparagus from just below the tip to the end with a peeler and trim off the woody end.
  - Wash the morels carefully to clear out the sand; cut them in half lengthways.
  - Roughly crush the hazelnuts.
technique: Peeling and cooking asparagus in butter and water, searing morels, a creamy sauce with cream and wine.
temperature: The asparagus simmers over low heat for 10–14 minutes until the tip of a knife goes in easily. The morels cook over medium heat for 6–8 minutes.
balance: The sweetness of the asparagus is balanced by the acidity of the wine and lemon, and the hazelnuts add crunch.
