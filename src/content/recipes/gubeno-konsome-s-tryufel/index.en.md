mise:
  - Soak the dried mushrooms in warm water for 20 minutes.
  - Finely chop the button mushrooms, shallot, carrot and celery.
  - Clean the fresh wild mushrooms with a brush.
technique: A consommé with an egg-white raft that clarifies the broth; searing mushrooms; a purée of Jerusalem artichoke.
temperature: The consommé simmers over low heat for 45–60 minutes without boiling; never stir once the raft has formed. The mushrooms are seared over high heat for 3–4 minutes.
balance: The earthiness of the mushrooms is balanced by the sweetness of the Jerusalem artichoke and the light butter.
