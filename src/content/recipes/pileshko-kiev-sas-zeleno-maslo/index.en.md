mise:
  - Mix soft butter with the garlic, the herbs, the lemon and the salt and shape it into a roll in cling film. Freeze for at least 1 hour.
  - "Prepare three shallow plates: one with flour, one with beaten eggs and one with breadcrumbs."
technique: Frozen butter is wrapped tightly in pounded chicken that is sealed all the way round so that nothing leaks. The double coating seals the roll, and finishing in the oven completes the cooking without burning.
temperature: Oil at 170 °C, 3–4 minutes on each side, then an oven at 180 °C for 10–12 minutes. The chicken is done at 74 °C in the middle.
balance: The chicken is neutral, the butter rich and fresh with garlic and lemon. The mash and the green beans balance the fat.

steps:
  - title: Pound the fillets
    text: Cut each breast open from the side without separating it, open it like a book and pound it between two sheets of cling film to a thickness of about 5 mm.
    why: An even thickness ensures even cooking.
  - title: Wrap the butter
    text: Cut the frozen butter into 4 sticks. Put one in the middle of each fillet, fold in the ends and roll up tightly, sealing the edges.
    avoid: If the edges are not sealed, the butter leaks out during frying.
  - title: Bread twice
    text: Roll in the flour, then in the egg, then in the breadcrumbs, then again in the egg and the breadcrumbs. Chill for 30 minutes.
    why: The double layer seals the roll and gives a crisp crust.
    time: 30
  - title: Fry
    text: Heat the oil to 170 °C and fry two Kievs at a time for 3–4 minutes on each side until golden.
    cue: The coating is evenly golden.
    time: 8
  - title: Finish in the oven
    text: Transfer to an oven at 180 °C for 10–12 minutes. The chicken is done at 74 °C in the middle.
    time: 12
  - title: Serve
    text: Leave for 2 minutes, then place on mashed potatoes with the green beans. Cut it open at the table so that the butter flows.
    time: 2
tips:
  - The butter must be frozen hard when stuffing.
  - If you have no thermometer, cut one piece open to check that it is done.
mistakes:
  - A soft filling – it leaks out during frying.
  - Oil that is too hot – the coating burns and the chicken stays raw.
substitutions:
  - Boneless chicken thighs instead of breasts.
  - Parmesan in the butter for a richer flavour.
plating: Two halves with the green butter visible on the mash, with green beans beside them.
serving: With mashed potatoes and green vegetables.
storage: Uncooked – up to 1 day in the refrigerator or a month in the freezer. Cooked ones are eaten immediately.
