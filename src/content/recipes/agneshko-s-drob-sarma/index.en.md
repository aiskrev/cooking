mise:
  - Take the leg of lamb out of the refrigerator an hour before roasting.
  - Cut the garlic into thick slivers and finely chop the spring onions and the herbs.
  - Wash the liver, remove the membranes and cut it into cubes of about 1 cm.
technique: Slow roasting under cover, which melts the connective tissue, then a short blast of heat at the end for the crust. The drob sarma bakes in a separate dish, and the topping goes on in the last minutes.
temperature: Oven at 160 °C (140 °C fan) for about 2 hours 20 minutes under foil, then 220 °C for 15–20 minutes without foil. For meat that falls away from the bone, the temperature in the thickest part is above 85 °C.
balance: Lamb is fatty and slightly sweet; the garlic, the rosemary and the yogurt on the side balance it. The spearmint is what makes drob sarma drob sarma – do not leave it out.

steps:
  - title: Prepare the ingredients
    text: Lay out the leg of lamb, the liver, the rice, the spring onions, the herbs, the eggs and the yogurt.
  - title: Season the lamb
    text: With the tip of a knife make deep incisions in the meat and push slivers of garlic and rosemary needles into them. Rub the whole leg with the salt, the black pepper and the oil.
    why: This way the garlic flavours the meat from the inside and does not burn on the surface.
  - title: Sear on all sides
    text: Heat the roasting tin on the hob over high heat and sear the leg on all sides, about 10 minutes in total.
    cue: The surface is evenly brown.
    time: 10
  - title: Roast slowly
    text: Pour the hot water into the tin, cover tightly with foil and roast for 2 hours 20 minutes at 160 °C. Remove the foil, raise the temperature to 220 °C and roast for another 15–20 minutes, basting twice with the juices from the tin.
    cue: The meat comes away from the bone under the pressure of a fork, and the crust is a dark golden colour.
    time: 160
  - title: Cook the base of the drob sarma
    text: About an hour before the end, heat the oil in a frying pan and fry the liver for 3–4 minutes. Add the spring onions and the rice and stir for 2 minutes. Add the parsley, the spearmint, the salt, the black pepper and the hot water, and transfer to a greased baking dish.
    why: Rice fried in the fat stays as separate grains.
  - title: Bake the drob sarma and add the topping
    text: Bake the drob sarma for 30 minutes next to the lamb, until the rice has absorbed the liquid. Whisk the eggs with the yogurt and the flour, pour over and return to the oven for 15 minutes, until the topping sets and forms a golden crust.
    cue: The rice is soft, and the topping is set and golden in patches.
    time: 45
  - title: Reduce the sauce
    text: Strain the juices from the lamb tin into a small saucepan, skim the fat from the surface and let it boil for a few minutes until it thickens.
    cue: The sauce coats the back of a spoon.
  - title: Rest and carve
    text: Cover the lamb loosely with foil and leave it for 20 minutes. Carve it across the grain or pull it into large pieces with a fork.
    why: During the rest the juices return to the centre and the meat stays succulent.
    time: 20
  - title: Serve
    text: Put a piece of lamb and a piece of drob sarma on each plate and spoon the sauce over the meat.
tips:
  - The tin must be tightly covered – if steam escapes, the meat dries out; if needed, add a little hot water.
  - Liver turns tough when it is cooked for a long time over high heat – in the pan it is only seared.
  - You can add other lamb offal to the liver, boiled in advance and cut up.
mistakes:
  - Carving straight after it comes out of the oven – the juices run out onto the board.
  - Pouring the topping over undercooked rice – the rice stays hard and the topping burns.
  - Using a lot of dried spearmint – it turns bitter; fresh is milder.
substitutions:
  - Lamb shoulder instead of leg – fattier and even more tender; the time is the same.
  - The rosemary can be replaced with fresh spearmint or thyme.
plating: A piece of lamb with the crust facing up, a square of drob sarma next to it, a spoonful of sauce over the meat and a spoonful of yogurt on the side.
serving: With a green salad of spring onions, radishes and cucumbers, and a glass of red wine.
storage: Up to 3 days in the refrigerator, the meat kept separate from the drob sarma. Reheat covered, with a spoonful of water, in an oven at 160 °C.
