mise:
  - Cut the meat into bite-sized pieces of 3–4 cm and pat dry.
  - Finely chop the onion, slice the spring onions into rings, and cut the herbs and the spinach into strips.
technique: The meat is browned and then tenderised under a lid; the greens go in for the last minutes so that they do not lose their colour; and the liaison is tempered with hot sauce so that the egg does not curdle.
temperature: Braise over low heat under a lid for 60–70 minutes. After the liaison goes in, the stew must not come to the boil.
balance: Lamb is fatty and slightly sweet; the green herbs freshen it, and the yogurt in the liaison rounds out the flavour.

steps:
  - title: Brown the meat
    text: Heat the oil in a pot over high heat and brown the lamb in two batches, 8–10 minutes in total, until it forms a brown crust.
    cue: The pieces are brown on all sides.
  - title: Braise
    text: Add the onion, the salt and the black pepper and cook for 5 minutes. Pour in the hot water, cover and let it simmer for 60–70 minutes over low heat.
    cue: The meat comes apart with a spoon.
    time: 65
  - title: Add the greens
    text: Add the spring onions, the spinach and the herbs and cook for another 5 minutes, until wilted.
    time: 5
  - title: Temper the liaison
    text: Whisk the egg with the yogurt and the flour. Gradually add a few spoonfuls of the hot sauce from the stew, stirring constantly, then pour the liaison into the pot.
    why: Warming it gradually keeps the egg from curdling.
    avoid: Do not let the stew boil after the liaison goes in.
  - title: Serve
    text: Leave for 5 minutes under the lid and serve in a deep plate, garnished with fresh herbs.
    time: 5
tips:
  - If you have no fresh herbs, use more spinach and a little dried dill.
  - The stew is at its best on the day it is made.
mistakes:
  - Cooking the greens for a long time – they lose their colour and aroma.
  - If the stew boils after the liaison goes in, the egg curdles.
substitutions:
  - Bone-in lamb – extend the braising by about 20 minutes.
  - Veal instead of lamb.
plating: In a wide white plate, the meat in a small mound in the sauce, with herbs and edible flowers on top.
serving: With bread and a spring salad.
storage: Up to 2 days in the refrigerator. Reheat over low heat without letting it boil.
