mise:
  - Take the meat out 30 minutes ahead, pat dry and remove the silverskin.
  - Season the meat with salt and crushed pepper on all sides.
  - Roast the beetroot in foil at 190 °C for 45 minutes, peel and cut into wedges.
  - Cut the celeriac into cubes and prepare the blackberries.
technique: Searing a game fillet with butter, roasting beetroot, puréeing celeriac, reducing a red wine sauce with juniper, blackberries for a sweet-and-sour accent.
temperature: Sear for 1.5–2 minutes on each side over high heat; finish at 180 °C for 5–8 minutes to 52–54 °C at the core; rest for 8–10 minutes (reaches 56 °C).
balance: Lean meat needs butter, sauce and purée. The acidity of the vinegar and the blackberries balances the gamey flavour.

tips:
  - The beetroot can be roasted the day before.
  - Do not overcook the meat – with game it is better pinker.
mistakes:
  - Slicing without a rest – the meat loses its juice.
  - A pan that is too hot – the butter burns.
substitutions:
  - Instead of venison – beef fillet or roe deer.
  - Instead of blackberries – blueberries or cherries.
plating: Purée at the base, sliced loin on it, beetroot and blackberries to the side, the sauce around.
serving: With a glass of full-bodied red wine.
storage: Best straight away; the purée and sauce keep for 2 days.
