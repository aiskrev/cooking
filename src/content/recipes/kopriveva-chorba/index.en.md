mise:
  - Pick the nettles wearing gloves and wash the tips in cold water.
  - Cut the meat into small cubes, the onion and the carrot finely and the potatoes into cubes about 1.5 cm long.
technique: The nettle is blanched for a minute in boiling water, which takes away its sting and excess bitterness, and is then chopped finely. It is added to the soup right at the end to keep its green colour.
temperature: The meat is boiled for 30–40 minutes at a gentle simmer. The nettle is blanched for 1–2 minutes and goes into the soup for the last 5.
balance: The nettle is earthy and lightly sweet, the yogurt on the side freshens it, and the dill and the parsley give it aroma.

steps:
  - title: Boil the meat
    text: In the pot, sweat the meat, the onion and the carrot in the oil for 5 minutes. Pour in the water, bring it to the boil and simmer gently for 30–40 minutes, skimming off the foam.
    cue: The meat comes apart with a fork.
    time: 35
  - title: Add the potatoes
    text: Add the potatoes and the salt and cook for another 15–20 minutes.
    time: 20
  - title: Blanch the nettle
    text: Drop the tips into boiling water for 1–2 minutes, drain them, rinse with cold water and chop them finely.
    why: Hot water destroys the stinging hairs; raw nettle is not eaten.
    avoid: Do not touch raw nettle with bare hands.
  - title: Add the greens
    text: Add the chopped nettle, the dill and half of the parsley to the soup and let it simmer for 5 minutes. Taste for salt.
    cue: The soup is a rich dark green.
    time: 5
  - title: Serve
    text: Ladle the soup into bowls, add a spoonful of yogurt and sprinkle with the remaining parsley.
tips:
  - Gather only young tips – older ones are fibrous.
  - The soup can also be made without meat, using vegetable stock.
mistakes:
  - Cooking the nettle for a long time – it loses its colour and aroma.
  - Raw nettle in the soup – stinging in the mouth.
substitutions:
  - Spinach or sorrel instead of nettle – with a shorter cooking time.
  - An egg whisked with yogurt (a liaison), as in the meatball soup.
plating: A deep white bowl, a spoonful of yogurt and finely chopped herbs on top.
serving: With fresh bread.
storage: Up to 2 days in the refrigerator. When reheating, do not let it boil, so that it does not lose its colour.
