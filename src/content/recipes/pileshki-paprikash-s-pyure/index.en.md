mise:
  - Pat the drumsticks dry and salt them 20 minutes before searing.
  - Cut the onion into thin half-moons, the peppers into strips and the garlic finely.
technique: The drumsticks are seared skin side down to a crisp crust, the onion and the peppers soften in the same fat, the paprika is added off the heat, and the cream finishes the sauce.
temperature: Sear over medium to high heat, then 30 minutes under a lid over low heat. The chicken is done at 74 °C in the thickest part.
balance: The paprika is sweet and warm, the wine and the tomato purée provide acidity, and the cream rounds it out. The sauce is light orange and smooth.

steps:
  - title: Sear the drumsticks
    text: Heat the oil in a wide frying pan. Sear the drumsticks skin side down for 6–7 minutes, turn them over and sear for another 3 minutes. Take them out.
    cue: The skin is golden and crisp.
  - title: Sweat the vegetables
    text: In the same fat sweat the onion for 5 minutes, add the peppers and the garlic and cook for another 5 minutes. Add the tomato purée and fry for 1 minute.
    time: 10
  - title: Add the paprika and the wine
    text: Take the pan off the heat, sprinkle in the paprika and immediately pour in the wine. Return to the heat and let it boil for 2–3 minutes.
    why: The liquid straight after it stops the paprika from burning and dissolves its aroma.
    avoid: Paprika burns quickly – it goes in off the heat.
  - title: Cook under a lid
    text: Return the drumsticks, pour in the stock, add the savory, cover and cook for 30 minutes over low heat.
    cue: The meat comes away easily from the bone.
    time: 30
  - title: Cook the mash
    text: While the chicken cooks, boil the potatoes in salted water for 20–25 minutes, rice them and stir in the butter and the hot milk until smooth.
    time: 25
  - title: Finish the sauce
    text: Take out the drumsticks, pour the cream into the sauce and let it simmer for 3–4 minutes until it thickens. Taste for salt.
    time: 4
  - title: Serve
    text: Spread the mash on a plate, arrange the drumsticks on it, spoon over the sauce and the peppers and garnish with a sprig of savory.
tips:
  - Drumsticks with skin give a richer sauce; if they are skinless, add a little butter.
  - The cream must not boil, otherwise the sauce curdles.
mistakes:
  - Burnt paprika – the sauce turns bitter.
  - Boiling cream – the sauce loses its smoothness.
substitutions:
  - Chicken breasts instead of drumsticks – cook them for 15 minutes under a lid.
  - Yogurt instead of cream – add it off the heat.
plating: A thin layer of mash, two drumsticks on it, the sauce and the peppers around them, a sprig of savory on top.
serving: With a green salad and a glass of white wine.
storage: Up to 2 days in the refrigerator. Reheat over low heat, the mash separately, with a little milk.
