mise:
  - Separate the whites from the yolks.
  - Switch the oven on to 180 °C and choose a frying pan that can go into it.
  - Finely chop the spring onion.
technique: The whipped whites are folded into the yolks in two stages and without hurry, so that the air does not escape. The omelette is cooked first on the hob to dry the underside, then in the oven to puff up.
temperature: Hob – 2 minutes over medium heat, then the oven at 180 °C for 6–8 minutes. The omelette must be cooked through, with no liquid yolk.
balance: The omelette is light and soft, the sauce rich and creamy. A little nutmeg and fresh spring onion tie them together.

steps:
  - title: Make the sauce
    text: Melt the butter, add the flour and stir for 1 minute. Add the warm milk in parts, stirring, and cook for 3–4 minutes until smooth. Add the cream, the nutmeg and the salt.
    time: 5
  - title: Whisk the yolks
    text: Whisk the yolks with the cream and a pinch of salt until smooth.
  - title: Whip the whites
    text: Whip the whites with a pinch of salt to stiff peaks in an absolutely clean, dry bowl.
    cue: The peaks stand when you turn the bowl over.
    avoid: A trace of fat or yolk keeps the whites from stiffening.
  - title: Fold
    text: Add a third of the whipped whites to the yolks and stir in. Add the rest in two additions, folding gently with a spatula from the bottom upwards.
    why: This way the air bubbles stay intact and the omelette puffs up.
  - title: Bake
    text: Heat the pan with the butter over medium heat, pour in the mixture, level it and cook for 2 minutes. Transfer to the oven for 6–8 minutes.
    cue: The omelette is puffed up, golden on top and springs back when touched.
    time: 8
  - title: Serve
    text: Slide the omelette onto a plate, fold it in half and pour the sauce over it. Sprinkle with spring onion and serve immediately.
tips:
  - Eggs at room temperature are whipped to peaks more easily.
  - Do not open the oven in the first 5 minutes.
mistakes:
  - Fast, vigorous stirring – the omelette falls.
  - Standing before serving – the soufflé shrinks.
substitutions:
  - Grated kashkaval in the sauce for a richer flavour.
  - Fresh herbs in the yolks.
plating: The folded omelette on a white plate, the sauce on top, spring onion, and drops of olive oil or herb butter on the side.
serving: Immediately, with a slice of toast and a salad.
storage: It does not keep – the omelette falls.
